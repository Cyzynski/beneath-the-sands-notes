The gang departed [[Hope's Embrace]] with the [[01. Convoy|Convoy]]. Along the way, they learned a little about [[Matthias Grubbinski]]'s family (sharing some "Bean Cake" with them) and even less about each other. After a largely uneventful journey, they arrived at [[Sand's End]], but not without first having a run in with some highwaymen that were all clad in uniforms with their insignia's torn off.
