After defeating the mechanical snake in the dungeon, the party fought about the crystals in the spell circle - Twinner wanted to give it his blood to make them light up, but Joseph and Simon vehemently opposed the thought. After some fighting, Twinner was able to illuminate 2/3 crystals. 

Then the gang worked to get the cows down from the floating ledge, misty-stepping with a mirror then feather-falling the cows down.

For their trouble, the Hensons rewarded them with 45 gold.
