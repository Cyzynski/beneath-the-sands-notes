# Backstory
Russell Stover (no relation) is one of many names used by the malevolent entropic force known as The Custodian. This being, usually in the guise of a low-level mortal spellcaster, exists only to clean up waste and detritus. This creature does not have a will of its own, but instead can be summoned by mortal spellcasters and assigned to tasks to "clean up".

Unfortunately, his most recent summons was the absolute skunk biscuit proprietor of Cornwell's Confections, Salacious Cornwell. This wizard, known for crafting whimsical, magical confections, embarked upon a vindictive bender after his birthday lava cake was destroyed, along with much of his candy store on the night before his birthday - which he would usually celebrate by throwing an extravagant party. He blamed the disaster on having outsourced some of the ingredients, and summoned The Custodian to clean up that monkey parade put on by those bloody vendors!"

The Custodian, not fully understanding Cornwell's colorful and figurative language, set out to clean up and collect every vendor remotely involved in the disastrous, elaborate cake recipe.

After this escapade, The Custodian spent several decades wandering the multiverse, carrying the souls of many sweet and ingredient magnates. *(Maybe a but if a stretch, but cogency isn't a priority.)*

So anyways, one of these magnates, the intergalactically ubiquitous Batter-witch herself, Betty Crocker, due to an integer overflow error in The Custodian's inventory of souls, has escaped, shunted out into the world of sands and steam, as a low-level sorceress hell-bent on finding the vile entity that had trapped her for so long so she can use it herself to return home and exact sweet vengeance on that bastard, Cornwell.

She was dropped into the desert (bakery and all) by an ethereal being - "Russel Stover (no relation)" after being plucked from her home. Many other "food mascots" were also dropped across the continent, including her now nemesis Mrs. Butterworth. She is hunting for "The Janitor" so she can get home.
