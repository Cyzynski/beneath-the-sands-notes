# Backstory
## Family History
Simon was born to a “new money” family in the Amber Palace (You or I can come up with a name, Simon changed his last name to hide). His grandparents/great aunts & uncles took advantage of the chaos during the expansion of the desert to forcefully gain control of the mine system of paragon. While not saints, his grandparents did put money into the town’s economy which allowed for expansion as well as the construction of Fort Pride to allow a place to rest while traveling between Paragon and Canningwood. His family is not necessarily broken up into families, but rather generations. His parents/aunts & uncles are basically raised as siblings and the same is true for his generation (you can add in family members if you like). The family is very “might makes right” and only uses weapons to fight due to their hatred of “Dirty Bastard Land”. This environment breeds a quickly growing extremism to be the best or do the most. This allowed Simon’s mother to rise to the top of her generation by strangling funds away from Paragon and later hiring a gang to terrorize the town. With the destruction of the mine she had lost favor with the family so a power struggle within her generation began which led to pressure being put on the next generation due to them nearly being of age to take over at the time.
## Personal History
His childhood was nothing particularly different from other members of his family. When he reached his teens combat training would begin but he would basically just half ass them so he could go back to the library in the Amber Palace. Many of the books were spoils of war from the north but in one of these he found a book that was the basics of spellcasting. He learned a few spells by copying them to small cards to keep them hidden but would only use mage armor before sparring with his family. After a while of doing this he was found out, his mother reprimanded him in a duel by taking a large chunk out of his ear and sent him back to his room. At this point Simon took his cards and escaped. He ended up at Hope’s Embrace where he gambled his way to basic gear and rations until he met up with the others.
