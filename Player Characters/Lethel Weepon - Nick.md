# Backstory
I’m from the Assassin’s Keep. I don’t know who my parents are and have no memories from before being there. I was raised as a protege of Vicious, the Master of Assassins. He leads the Assassins Keep and is an extremely dangerous and powerful man. I have never questioned him and have respect for him as my Master. Because I was raised there from a young age I never learned the normal joys of life. Anything that could hinder my mission’s success was trained out of me including emotions and attachments of any kind. I have carried out countless flawless missions and am becoming notorious myself, garnering fear even from my fellow assassins. I have a robotic personality and have been raised to be the ultimate tool of the Assassins Organization. I have never flinched at killing even when my victim begs for their life. That is, until one particular experience recently. My mission was to kill a young child destined to inherit a minor seat of nobility, and make it look like an accident. While I was in the act the child’s mother burst into the room. I immediately threw a knife into her throat to silence her but through gurgles of blood she quietly begged me to spare her child saying to take him anywhere, just let him live. I initially ignored her please and went to finish the job, but the mother’s final sobs caused me to flinch. My involuntary flinch was so out of the ordinary for me it gave me pause. I don’t know what overcame me but I decided the job would still be complete if the child didn’t inherit the seat of nobility so I disposed of the mothers body and dropped the child off at a small farm far away. I covered it up making it look like the mother and child fled an unpleasant father. The child’s name is Arthur. Since this incident I have had a small seed of doubt as to the orders I’ve been given, what the heaviness my chest is, and what the odd feelings I’ve had ever since the incident have been.

I came on this trip with Grubbinski under orders from Vicious to go to the desert and learn about the ruins and what potential new weapons could be discovered from them like the guns were (or potential improved types of guns). I am following those orders but also using this long trip away from the Assassin’s Keep to evaluate my feelings and try to decide if I want to keep following order blindly forever or not.
# Long Term Goal
To decide he doesn’t want to keep following orders and take down Vicious or at least defect from the Assassin’s Organization.
