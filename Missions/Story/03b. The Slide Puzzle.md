# The Pit
Looking down...
>The pit descends sharply almost 50 feet. At the center of the depression is a metal ladder leading deeper into the rocky surface below.

Descending the pit requires either a rope to follow down or a DC 10 Acrobatics check to avoid tumbling to the bottom (and taking some fall damage along the way).

## Slide Puzzle
At the end of the hallways that go to the end of each "tile" of the ruin, is a Compass-like shape. Pressing the symbol on the star slides the tile on the opposite side of [[#The "Centroid of the Mechanism", if you will]] in the indicated direction, covering it.

For example, in the starting setup shown in the image below, clicking the bottom symbol would cause the tile with [[#Chamber 1]] to slide down, into the center space.

![[WallConsole.png|200]]

If a character wishes to jump from one tile to another while it is in motion or if they are "squished" by a room sliding into them. They must make a *Dexterity saving throw*: DC 15. *Failure*: the creature takes 3d8 force damage and is forced to either side (GM's discretion). *Success*: the creature chooses what side of the divide to move to.
## Anti-Magic Zones
The center space of the tile-sliding-area contains an anti-magic zone. All constructs in the area "turn off", spells cannot be cast or affect the area within, and magical items function like their mundane counterparts.
## The Ladder
> The ladder descends into a long, hallway (really more of a tall metal tube)- about 50 feet long and gently sloping upwards. As your feet hit the ground, the reverberating sound of metal fills the air. Each footfall echoing into the expanse.

At the end of the hall is another ladder, also leading down.

>At the bottom of the second ladder is another hallway, similar to the first, but this one seems level. It is jutting off at a slightly askew angle. from the base of the ladder.

This ladder drops into [[#Chamber 1]]. These halls rotate along each other such that they always open into Chamber 1 while still leading up and out.
## The "Centroid of the Mechanism", if you will
The whole dungeon is suspended over a *deeeeeep* hole, but stays afloat thanks to some arcane crystals suspending each individual room. The first time the party sees the hole left by the missing square, read the following:

> A yawning hole opens beneath, going deeper than eye can see. A faint thrum and glow emanate from beneath the dungeon, around the chasm.

If the party insists on going down, it leads to the underdark or something. That'll require some rework - but could be neat.
## Chamber 1
>The ladder descends into a 40-foot square room. A smattering of (SPACE FILLER) dot the room. The walls feature a repeating illustration of a 3x3 grid of squares but the center icon is a blue wavy circle instead.

When a room slides out of position, the pattern changes. The "wavy circle" is always where the gap is. The center space is always blue (it's where the anti-magic zone is.)

**Treasure:** 2d6 GP, 2d8 SP, 3d10 CP all of an ancient and unique minting. (It's Zmokan currency. It spends just as well as the modern stuff but some people might pay more for it...)
## Chamber 2
*Lab 1*

A messy lab filled with dozens of spears, swords, and other melee weaponry. Each weapon is inscribed with arcane sigils and animates when a player enters the room.

**Enemies.** [[Animated Armory]]

**Treasure.** A whole 'lotta damaged melee weapons.
## Chamber 3
*Lab 2*

A messy lab with tables covered in partially constructed mechanical doo-dads.

**Enemies.** A [[Spellguard]] that's a construct instead of humanoid and two [[Copper Golem]]s

**Treasure.** *Immovable Rod*, and a wind up toy that looks like a miniature Copper Golem.
## Chamber 4
*Secret Chamber*

A bunch of clockwork golems guard a vault in the center of the room. The vault is surrounded by glowing, arcane chains that bind it to the floor. The golems are standing, holding hands, in a circle around the vault - facing outward.

**Enemies.** 15 [[Copper Golem]]. One of which is wearing a patchy wizard hat and wielding a [[Wand of Magic Missiles]].

**Treasure.** A [[Ring of Chronal Dilation]] and 2 [[Bead of Mist]] are locked in the vault. If this room's tile is in the anti-magic zone, the chains become nonmagical and the lock binding them can be picked (DC 18).

## Chamber 5
*Housing*

Cubicles containing beds and small dressers. One of the cubicles contains a chalkboard featuring a drawing of a octagonal room with two entrances.

**Treasure.** One dresser contains a [[Pearl of Power]], another has some [[Eternal Chalk]].

## Chamber 6
*Practice Room*

**Enemies.** [[Training Dummy]] and two [[Enhanced Liquimetal Flying Weapon]]s.

**Treasure.** Something fun and possibly quirky, and a [[Bead of Mist]]

## Chamber 7
*Experimentation Room*

Some sort of mechanically and magically enhanced corpse creature (the Imbued) in a tube. 

**Enemies.** An [[Imbued]] imprisoned and seemingly unconscious in the tube. If this room is ever moved into the center tile of the dungeon or if the glass is broken, the creature in the tube breaks free and will hunt any living creatures alive in the dungeon. 

**Treasure.** The key to [[#Chamber 8]] and 2 *Potions of Greater Healing*.
## Chamber 8
*Boss Chamber*

**Enemies.** [[Arc Walker]] and two [[Copper Golem]]s. The golems will use Spark to repair the Arc Walker as they are able, but they'll probably each be destroyed before they even get a turn.

**Treasure.** The artifact piece.
