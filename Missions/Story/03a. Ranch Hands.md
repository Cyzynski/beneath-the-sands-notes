# Preamble
In the desert, at L5, the Henson Ranch was split in half when the ground beneath it lifted off the ground during the sandstorm.
# At the Ranch
As the party approaches
>As you draw nearer to the hovering mass of rock, it becomes more and more apparent that it isn't just some floating rock... there's a barn on top. Well... half a barn.

When the party arrives:
>On the ground by the other half of the barn stands a beleaguered man frantically trying to assemble a fence along the crater left behind when the massive chunk of rock chose to lift up out of the ground. A faint glowing emanates from the bottom of the crater. A woman on horseback calmly circles the currently free cows, coaxing them to stay put.
>
>**Steve:** (Panting from exertion) *"Is that all of them? I thought I saw another wander off to the West."*
>
>**Gayle:** *"Yes, Steve. I got 'em all... except the ones still up top."*
>
>**S**: (Scoffs) *"Don't remind me... There's no way we're getting those back. And poor Thaddeus!!"*

The people are [[The Hensons]]. Thaddeus is their prized bull (and aggressive). The rock on which half the farmhouse floats is 100 feet over the surface of the desert. On top of the island are Frank (in a sectioned off pen, alone) as well as 2 cows and 2 calves.

The Hensons will pay the party 5 gold for each cow retrieved from the top - and an additional 15 if they can get Thaddeus.
# The Pit
Looking down...
>The pit descends sharply almost 50 feet. At the center of the depression is an understated circular stone stairway deeper into the rocky surface below.

Descending the pit requires either a rope to follow down or a DC 10 Acrobatics check to avoid tumbling to the bottom (and taking some fall damage along the way).
# The Ruins
The spiral stairs from the pit descend into the center of [[#Chamber 1]]. The three rings in the dungeon can be rotated independently from one another using the device found in Chamber 1.

![[CircleDungeon.png|600]]
*(Each square is 10'x10')*
## Chamber 1
*"The Entrance"*
>The tight spiral staircase descends into a circular room, at the center of which is a small plinth. On top of that plinth rests a gauntlet made of an iridescent metal. Three rings encircle the gauntlet, increasing in size as they move away from the hand. A fourth, smaller, ring encircles the thumb.

The inscription on the plinth is as follows:
![[ArtifactPlinth.png]]

The gauntlet is *The Burden of Authority*. Close inspection of it will reveal traces of dried blood inside. 
* Putting on or taking off the gauntlet subjects the creature to a *Constitution saving throw*: DC 18. *Failure*: The creature takes 2d6 necrotic damage. *Success*: Half damage.
* While equipped, the wearing creature can use an action to rotate one of the three rings suspended around *The Burden*, rotating the associated ring of the ruin. 
	* If a creature is in a space split by the rotation, they must make a *Dexterity saving throw*: DC 15. *Failure*: the creature takes 3d8 force damage and is forced to either side. *Success*: the creature chooses what side of the divide to move to.
* The thumb ring is used to control the brightness of the arcane lights positioned throughout the ruin - which are off by default.
* The gauntlet cannot be removed from the ruins. A *Wall of Force*-style barrier blocks it from crossing the threshold at the top of the stairs. If it is being worn by a creature that crosses over that threshold, it is immediately torn off. The wearer immediately fails the save against the damage if this happens.

## Chamber 3
*"The Abbot's Office"*
>A desk sits opposite a couple of chairs. In the corner of the room is a small dresser.

The dresser contains a [[Robe of the Clergy]] in a crumpled heap at the bottom. The desk is conspicuously empty other than a strange, notched, circular piece of metal *(Key to [[#Chamber 8]])* and a forgotten partial bottle of some wine that has gone sour with age.
## Chamber 4
*"The Library"*
>The hallway opens into a massive room surrounded and filled with bookshelves and scroll racks - almost entirely empty. Three abstract metal statues stand guard of the empty library.

If anyone not wearing a [[Robe of the Clergy]] or *The Burden of Authority* enters the library, they are immediately attacked by the three [[Blade Construct]]s.

One spellbook is on the shelf by the hallway containing [[#Chamber 7]]. Pulling on the book reveals the secret hall. The spellbook contains the following:
* [[Dragon Claw]]
* [[Ricochet]]
* [[Rustwind]]

The scroll rack contains a *[Scroll of](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9229085-spell-scroll?srsltid=AfmBOoorgbI0-ZIO1On7M5XgP6SVZ8NxWIpkaTveLtnDoV6GbM3oilme) [Prayer of Healing](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Prayer%20of%20Healing#content)* and a *Scroll of [[Starfall]]*.
## Chamber 5
*"The Workshop"*
>Unlike the rest of this underground maze, this room isn't neat and tidy. Workbenches fill the space, covered in partially constructed devices and materials.

The first time the party enters this room, three [[Scavenger]]s rise up from the workbenches and attack anyone not wearing the [[Robe of the Clergy]] or *The Burden of Authority* unless they attack it first. 

Two of the scavengers contain *Charged Crystals* (when cracked/shattered, they explode like [[Equipment#Dynamite|Dynamite]]), which well detonate upon its destruction. The third scavenger contains an uncommon [[Battle Furnace]].

## Chamber 7

## Chamber 8
*"The Vault"*
The two keys (from [[#Chamber 3]] and [[#Chamber 9]]) must be used, one in each doorway, to open the doors to this room.

>The ceiling of this massive stone chamber is easily twice that of the other rooms you've seen in this facility so far. Two rows of pillars support the earth above. In the center of the room is a mechanical snake over 15 feet in length coiled around a small plinth. A small contraption sits on top of the plinth.

Room contains [[Clockwork Serpent]] and one part of some sort of 3-part device (the others will be in [[03b. The Slide Puzzle]] and [[03c. Wibbly Wobbly Timey Wimey]]).
## Chamber 9
*"Upper Dormitory"*
>Several well made beds sit in individual cubicles in this space with small chests at their feet. A private bathroom off to one side. 

Opening the chest containing the key to open [[#Chamber 8]] causes two [[Ripper Drone]]s to emerge. The other chests contain 2d8 GP each.

## Chamber 12
*"The Kitchen"*
>A large table fills the center of this room. A stovetop and oven fill the walls next to another small door.

The room contains enough pieces of cookware to comprise two sets of Cook's Utensils. The closet in the corner is empty save for a [Heward's Handy Spice Pouch](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wondrous-items:hewards-handy-spice-pouch).
## Chamber 13
*"The Danger Hall"*
When the party enters the hallway, they are greeted by this sign:
![[FirstHallwaySign.png]]

Immediately after which, is a trap. Then there are many, many other traps. Each has a discovery DC of 15. Use some from below:

* **Blast Trap**: *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 12. *Failure*: 3d6 Bludgeoning damage *Success*: Half damage. (The trap can be disarmed to yield a crystal that functions as 1 stick of [[Equipment#Dynamite|Dynamite]], except it deals Force damage).
