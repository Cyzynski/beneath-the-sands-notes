> **H:** *"Sorry Rex, Sheriff's still out and I can't leave the town unprotected. Lets get you to the priest."*

(This is where the party steps up... hopefully)

The mining company has some extra horses in camp - allow each player to describe what their horse looks like.

The horses are spooked by all of the commotion and yelling, getting a horse to allow a player to mount requires a **DC 13 Animal Handling** check. Bribing the horse with a snack reduced the DC to 10. Failing the check results in the DC to increase by 2.
# The Ride
>Harold is waiting for you at the gate.
>
>**H:** *"The dig site is a few hours ride {to the Northwest}. May Providence guide you."*
>
>He hands you a small pouch as your group takes off through the Dust Gate at a full gallop towards the dig site.

The pouch contains two *Potions of Healing*.

When the party leaves town, establish marching order. It'll be important later!

If the party is pushing the horses (i.e. making them dash each round), Animal Handling checks may be required to maintain control.

# The Dig Site
The party starts 600ft down and to the left of the below map. All rock ledges are 10 feet high and can be climbed with a successful DC 13 athletics check.

>A lone sniper stands atop rickety wooden scaffold overlooking a rocky ledge. Near the watchtower are a couple of tents, billowing in the breeze as well as a stack of crates.
>
>Heavily armed men swarm the site, getting ready to... celebrate... your arrival.

**Enemies:** 1 [[Highwayman Sniper]] stands on top of the watchtower. 2 [[Highwayman|Highwaymen]] and a [[Highwayman Heavy]] guard the upper level. Another [[Highwayman]] and a [[Highwayman Slugger]] wait in the lower level before the entrance to the ruins.

![[DigSite.jpg|571]]
## Lower level
>A basin sits at the bottom of the cliff face, at the bottom of which 2 highwaymen patrol - one of which is armed with a shotgun. 
>
>Bullet-ridden corpses scatter the area.
>
>A few crates and empty flasks are scattered around the area. Screams and other sounds of panic emanate from within a charred-edged, blasted-open hole in the basin leading down into darkness.

The hole leads down into the ruins after a 20 foot drop. A cut rope sits spooled at the bottom.
### Enemies
A [[Highwayman]] and a [[Highwayman Slugger]] guard the area. Each of which carry the standard loot as well as a [[Vial of Embers]]. One of them also has a small, crude drawing of a family folded up in his pocket.
### Treasure
* The crates contain a couple of pick axes, unlit torches, a tinderbox, several flasks (of water), and 1 *Potion of Healing*.
* A smaller box gently nestled among some packing materials contains 3 sticks of [[Equipment#Dynamite|Dynamite]]. Striking the box will cause it to detonate.
## Upper Level
>The tall watchtower overlooks the rest of the dig site, towering over a small stack of create near the edge. Two tents stand sentinel near the tower. One flaps wildly in the breeze, its canvas in tatters where a corpse has fallen through it. 
>
>In addition to the sniper in the tower, 3 highwaymen guard the area, one wields a crank-operated volley gun, mounted on a turret, overlooking the rest of the dig site.
### Enemies
2 [[Highwayman|Highwaymen]] and a [[Highwayman Heavy]] guard the area.
### Treasure
* The crates contain 15 days of water and provisions. 
* Each tent has 3 tussled bedrolls, the less-tattered tent also contains a backpack with two partial bottles of fine whiskey.
* The top of the watchtower has a backpack with 50 feet of rope and a grappling hook.

When venturing into this room, some poor sap triggered an explosive trap, collapsing the ceiling.
## Chamber 2
> Small hieroglyphic-like figures are drawn in a band around the room, each of them holds a rectangular tablet over their heads.
## Chamber 3
If the guards weren't lured away.
>Three highwaymen sit against the walls of the room, exhausted. Two ruined spherical constructs spark on the floor.

* **Insight 10+:** the highwaymen look unhappy - they definitely didn't expect this to be as hard as it was.
* **Medicine 10+:** the highwaymen are wounded, one of which has been severely. 

Regardless:
> The figures on the walls in this room are replaced with small circles with detailed gold leaf detailing.
> 
> A lever protrudes from each of the walls of the room. The one on the North wall is in the down position, the other three are up.
### Puzzle
Unless all levers are simultaneously moved to the down position, when any is moved, 2 [[Clockwork Drone]]s drop from the ceiling into the room, attacking whoever pulled the levers. Once all levers are in the down position, the [[#Secret Door]] opens.

The highwaymen pulled the lever on the North wall, causing a drone to spawn - they will try to convince the party not to pull any of the levers (they're scared of them). 
### Secret Door
The door to [[#Chamber 5]] is hidden. A DC 15 perception check is required to spot it. 
## Chamber 4
>This large, domed room features a dusty altar on a raised dais in the center, the illustrated figures on the walls are all on their knees, arms outstretched to either side.

**Investigation 13+:** There's a small rectangle in the center of the altar free of dust.

## Staying Behind
If the party chooses to stay behind, a [[Broodwarden]] *(Thunder resistance)* busts through the floor with a scream and begins to tear into whoever is closest.

It attacks wildly, going for walls, supports, etc. It might make the entrance collapse.
## Riders of the Storm
If the party chooses to go back with Helena, they must make 5 DC 10 Constitution Saves along their way back, taking 1d4 slashing damage on a failure. After the third, a [[Sound Dragon Wyrmling]] strikes. Each round, the players must make a DC 10 Con save or take 1d4 slashing damage.
