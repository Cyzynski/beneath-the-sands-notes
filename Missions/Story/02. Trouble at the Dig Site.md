# Preamble
Later in the afternoon (or perhaps the evening) following the arrival of the party in [[Sand's End]]:
> A clatter of hooves pounds into town. 
> 
> **?:** *"Help! Bandits have seized the dig site!"*
> 
> (if inside: You step outside to see...)
> 
> A deputy standing by the now open Dust Gate, keeping a tentative watch - but definitely paying more attention to the commotion in the town square.
>  
> [[Sheriff's Office#Deputies|Harold]] scrambles down from his watch-post atop the Dirt Gate and rushes over to the rider, a slight middle-aged human man with a distant look in his eyes; blood creeps out from his armpit, spreading down both sides of his shirt.
> 
> **H:** *"[[Dust Lake Mining Company#Employees|Rex]]! What's happened!"*
> 
> **R:** (breathing heavily) *"They've taken everyone! They got us with our skivvies 'round our ankles! You know me, I ain't yeller-bellied - but I knew we were outgunned, so I high-tailed it back here to rally the troops. Bastards were taking pot shots and one of 'em got lucky"*
> 
> **H:** *"Sorry Rex, Sheriff's still out and I can't leave the town unprotected. Lets get you to the doc."*

(This is where the party steps up... hopefully)

The mining company has some extra horses in camp - allow each player to describe what their horse looks like.

The horses are spooked by all of the commotion and yelling, getting a horse to allow a player to mount requires a **DC 13 Animal Handling** check. Bribing the horse with a snack reduced the DC to 10. Failing the check results in the DC to increase by 2.
# The Ride
>Harold is waiting for you at the gate.
>
>**H:** *"The dig site is a few hours ride {to the Northwest}. May Providence guide you."*
>
>Your group takes off through the Dust Gate at a full gallop towards the dig site.

When the party leaves town, establish marching order. It'll be important later!

If the party is pushing the horses (i.e. making them dash each round), Animal Handling checks may be required to maintain control.
# The Arrival
As the party approaches (600 ft away), a [[Highwayman Sniper]] attacks whoever is in the front *(the attack will be made at disadvantage for range)*. 

{[Horse Stats](https://www.aidedd.org/monster/riding-horse)} See [[Mounted Combat]] for information on how to handle this.

**On Hit:**
>An explosion rings out. A few heartbeats later **{NAME}**, a piercing sensation tears through your shoulder as a bullet does the same.

**On Miss:**
>An explosion rings out. A few heartbeats later, a cloud of dust kicks up less than a foot away from **{NAME}**'s horse.

At this point, roll initiative!
