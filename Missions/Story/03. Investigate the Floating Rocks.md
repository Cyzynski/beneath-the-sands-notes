# Sandstorm Attack
On the way back to town after the party has secured two of the device pieces, they will be attacked.

>Without warning, a sandstorm is suddenly upon you. It wasn't on the horizon ahead of you - the weather went from clear to an instantaneous torrent of grit. 

Everyone must make a *Constitution Saving Throw*: DC 12. 
* *Failure*: 1d4 Slashing damage and Blinded until they take an action to clear their eyes.
* *Success*: Half damage only.

For those that aren't Blinded:
>Through the gloom of the swirling sands, you pick out the approaching forms of 2 masked Dragonborn. The sands seem to flow *around* instead of *into* them. 
>
>**?**: *"Cease your investigations at once. You know not what you are unearthing. Give us the artifact and you may return home."*

The dragonborn are [[Way of the Sands Monk]]s. When they become bloodied, they will use their Reactions to dissipate into sand and flee, at which point, the storm will die down. If they render the party unconscious, they will leave the party's bodies and take the artifact to a mysterious location TBD.
