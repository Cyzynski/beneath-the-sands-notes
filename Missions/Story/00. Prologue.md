# Preamble
>Our story begins in the sea-side city of [[Leafall]], at [[Grove University]]. One [[Dr. Malcom Doppler]] is putting together an expedition deep into the untamed wilds of the [[Southern Expanse]]. One of his students, [[Helena Cartier]], has unearthed some new information that could be used to prove the existence of the mythical Zmoka people. Dr. Doppler put out the call for both muscle and research aides. Whether it be for gold, glory, or class credit, you have heeded his call and congregated in his office at GU. From behind a small podium - entirely unnecessary, given the size of the room - Dr. Doppler addresses the room.
>
>**D:** *"Thank you all for coming today. We stand on the precipice of true greatness - a discovery that will make all other pale in comparison! Now, I could go on and on about all what exactly it is that we're going to be doing out there in the wilds, but I do think Miss Cartier is much more qualified to do so, so without further ado, I present to you all, our expedition leader!"* 
>
>With an over-the-top flourish, he raises his hand, and gestures to a young, awkward-looking elven woman. She awkwardly takes his place behind the podium.
>
>**H:** *"Thank you... uh... for that... introduction, Doctor"* She awkwardly wrings her hands as she looks up from the podium. *"And thank .. you all for .. um .. coming with on this expedition .. to discover that which has never been seen before! Together, we will do the impossible! We will finally prove the existence of the [[Zmoka]]!"*
>
> From what you all know, the Zmokan people are the stuff of fairy tales, often used as bedtime stories for children. Very little is known about them, however, almost all stories agree on two specific details
> 1. They were experts at infusing machinery with magic
> 2. They worshiped the god Ambition... until they killed him
> 
> Everything else varies from story to story depending on the "moral" the bard is trying to weave.
>
> **D:** (chuckles nervously and rubs his hands together) *"I had anticipated revealing the nature of our sought after discovery in a less... fanatical... way. Welp, now that the cat's out of the bag, do you have any questions for us?"*

After adequate discussion...
> **D:** *"It seems the time for discussion of our grand plans has passed. So let us go! Our train awaits! I will see you all at the train station for our departure in...*(checks his watch)*... oh deer. It seems as though I have let the time get away from me. I do so love discussing the finer points of a plan, but alas! The destiny - and to a lesser extent, the railway - waits for no man!*

# The Train Trip
## After arriving at the train station
> *"All aboard!"* a voice yells from one of the passenger cars. Dr. Doppler hands each of you a ticket.
> 
> **D:** *"We have compartments three and four, please make yourselves comfortable. If you would like to join me for some truly stimulating conversation, I intend to sit in three. Helena -"*
> **H:** *"I have to study for arrival."* she blurts out. *"..um... sorry, doctor. I just... want to be sure I don't lead us astray out there. It is unmapped terrain after all."*
> 
> The doctor adopts a knowing smile, a touch of sadness behind his eyes.
> 
> **D:** *"I understand Helena, say no more. You carry much on your shoulders."* He pauses a moment, during which Helena rushes onto the train, then he claps his hands together and adopts a smile that doesn't quite reach his eyes. *"Welp, my offer extends to the rest of you. I will see you aboard!"*

Each car can seat 4 average-sized individuals comfortably. The party will have to split themselves between them.
## In Dr. Doppler's Car
> When you enter, Dr. Malcom Doppler is reading a thick tome entitled *Gadgets and Gizmos Throughout the Modern Ages: Exposed, Unpacked, and Declassified*.
> 
> **D:** *"Oh hello there! Welcome!"* He eagerly closes his book and sets it aside before looking up at you. *"Are you ready for our expedition into the uncharted reaches of the Southern Expanse? I can scarcely fathom that the day of our departure is at hand! Well, we can't very well risk life and limb in lands unknown for each other if we don't know the other's innermost selves. Please, tell me, why did you agree to this adventure?"* The whole time he is speaking, he is busing himself pulling a variety of snacks from his bag and setting them up on the table between you.
## In Helena's Car
> As you enter, you see Helena has already claimed the entirety of the table for her various notes. The small space feels all the smaller given the mess. Leaning over the table, Helena looks up at you, hair frizzy and a pencil in her mouth. 
>
> **H:** *"Oh hi... um.... sorry, about taking up all the space... Uh... make yourselves comfortable, I guess."*
>
> Pleasantries finished, she goes back to rifling through her notes, muttering about canyons and avoiding wildlife.
>
>**H:** *"Have any of you ever killed a theropod? I saw a note somewhere that mentioned someone claiming they saw one in the region we'll be exploring. Of course, I'm sure that individual was probably just suffering from heatstroke, I highly doubt that anyone who writes academic papers for a living is capable of slaying much anything - the doctor and I certainly aren't - well, I suppose he may have a gadget of some kind or another up his sleeve..."* (Trailing off and realizing she's been rambling) *"... sorry... um... have you ever seen a theropod?"*
# Hex Crawl
![[Ruins_1_Hexcrawl.png|400]]
In the map above, the lime-edged hexes are 100ft tall cliff faces. The ruins are located in the shaded hex, on the southern edge. When the party arrives, they start in the second grassland hex at (0, 3). The party can travel 3 hexes a day, 4 if they risk a level of exhaustion. Moving through a mountain takes twice as long as a normal hex. *Jungle Hill* hexes are clifftops, the others are "ground level" with smooth transitions between except the cliff faces. Each new hex the party moves into, roll a d20, on 16+ pick one of the encounters below at random.

## Elemental Circle
> You see four square patterns on the ground, arranged equidistantly around a carved circle. One square is charred, the next is a small pool of water, the third is a dirt mound, and the fourth is a metal cage that seems to be crackling with electricity.
>
> In the center lies a small glowing gem.

Stepping into the circle causes the gem to glow and then explode, dealing 3d6 Piercing and 3d6 Thunder damage to all creatures in a 40-foot radius that fail a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw.

## Dilapidated Well
> In a large clearing, a pile of rocks defines a crude well. Covered in moss, the well smells of roses and you note a slight chill in the air.
> 
>Approaching the well, a feeling of desperate thirst washes over you. A **[Water Weird](https://www.aidedd.org/monster/water-weird)** springs from the well to attack anyone that gets too close.

## Termite Nest
> The ground underfoot is dry, the air lacks any humidity, and the grass is brown from a lack of water. Rising up in front of you, almost 15 feet high, stands the biggest termite mound you have ever seen.
>
> The ground begins to to shake and the mound crumbles as a **[Bulette](https://www.aidedd.org/monster/bulette)** breaks through and charges you!

The party is Surprised by this, so they have disadvantage on their initiative rolls.

# Ruins
## Exterior
The ruins lie buried within a fertile valley in the Southern Expanse. Its entrance is almost completely buried beneath a mix of fallen stone and overgrown foliage.
![[Ruins_1_Entrance.png|300]]
When the characters arrive, characters with a passive perception of 13+ notice the entrance.
> In the face of the stone cliff, nestled amidst the layers of limestone, sandstone, and shale, you see what's left of the entrance to some sort of subterranean structure.

Clearing the entrance by hand takes 10 hours. Each creature that succeeds on a DC 13 Strength check decreases this time by 1 hour. After 5 hours, the party is attacked by 4 **[Allosaurus](https://www.aidedd.org/monster/allosaurus)**. Make a shared stealth roll for the dinosaurs against the party members' passive perception. If all of the allosauruses succeed against a character, that character is Surprised *(they have disadvantage on initiative)*.
![[Ruins_1_Entrance_Attack.png|300]]
> A branch snaps and suddenly, crashing through the tree line are three, 20 foot long bipedal lizards. The one in the lead throws its head back and lets out an earthshaking [roar](https://youtu.be/g3rIFjCVawc?si=rHeMPLPJWgjsyjc3).
## Interior
*(North is in the top right)*
![[Ruins_1_Map.png]]
The entirety of the ruins are dark.
### Chamber 1
At the top of the stairs...
> Just inside the colonnades of the entrance is a stairwell descending deeper into the rock below. The walls are made of large red terracotta bricks. Along the wall at about shoulder height is a lighter colored stripe depicting a line of crude hieroglyphic-like drawings depicting figures, arms outstretched, each holding a cube. The figures all look like they're walking deeper into the ruin. An unlit torch sits in a sconce on the right wall.

After descending...
> As the stairs end, a room opens. The space is rectangular and moderately sized, its floor tiled. A hallway opens up to the North. In the center of the room is an ornate rug, weathered by time. A pile of rubble sits in the corner.

Characters with a passive Perception of 13+ notice a strange tile:
>At a cursory glance, you notice one of the tiles looks... odd.

The odd-looking tile is a pressure plate, there is another one under the rubble. When **both** pressure plates have a person standing on them, a loud grinding sound echoes through the cavern and the room shakes as the secret door to room 5 opens. The door will only remain open as long as both pressure plates are pressed.

The door slams shut immediately if any pressure plates are released. If a creature is standing in the doorway when this happens, they must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (2d10) bludgeoning damage

**Enemies:** The rug is a **[Rug of Smothering](https://www.aidedd.org/dnd/monstres.php?vo=rug-of-smothering)** and will attack any creature that gets too close to it.
### Chamber 2
> This small room contains nothing but dust and a few earthenware pots in one corner. The glyph figures in this room all hold vases over their heads. 

One of the figures has dropped his vase. Characters with a passive Perception of 13+ notice this.

**Treasure:** The pots contain some loot.
1. 11 copper pieces of a unique minting, featuring gear detailing on one side and a roaring dragon on the other. 
2. A rusted pair of spectacles missing a lens alongside some rusty nails. The glasses can be worn to see the safe path drawn on the ceiling of [[#Chamber 3]].
3. A potion of healing - if this pot is smashed, the potion is as well.
### Chamber 3
> The hall opens into a vast square room. The illustrated figures on the walls are depicted with their spears in mid throw.
>
> Across the chamber, a pair of double doors inset with gems and brass gears lie closed.

There are pressure plates on all of the floor except the tiles marked on the map. Each time a creature steps on a space not part of the trail, a **[[Clockwork Drone]]** (or 2?) drops from the ceiling along the edge of the room.

The safe path across the room is drawn on the ceiling above, a character wearing the glasses from [[#Chamber 2]] can see it.

Once Dr. Doppler has a chance to examine a Drone...
>**D:** (Picking up a the remnants of a drone) *"Fascinating! How **do** they make this work?"*
>
>He begins to gather up remnants of the drone, putting them into his bag.
### Chamber 4
> Across from the entrance is a raised dais with an altar with a plate, on top of which is a brass key. Standing in the corners of the room are humanoid gearwork statues, armed with greatswords.

**Enemies:** When the key is removed from the altar, the weapons carried by the statues animate as five **[[Liquimetal Flying Weapon]]s**.

### Chamber 5
> The air here is stale and a cloud of dust fills the air. Inside, the walls are more of the same, except here, the glyph figures are holding what looks to be swords and are standing at attention. The tile in the northwest corner of the room looks crooked. In the northwest corner is a small, wooden chest that looks withered with age.

When the pressure plate in this room is pressed in conjunction with the two in [[#Chamber 1]], the door to [[#Chamber 6]] scrapes and rumbles open - but only so long as all the plates are all pressed. 

**Enemies:** The first time all three plates are pressed in unison, 1 **[[Force Construct]]** drop from the ceiling near each of the three plates, attacking whoever is on it, trying to make them move off - they single-mindedly target any creature standing on one of the plates. The "corpses" of the animated armors can be used to hold down the pressure plates if need be. 

The door slams shut immediately if any pressure plates are released. If a creature is standing in the doorway when this happens, they must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (2d10) bludgeoning damage

**Treasure:** Inside the chest is an iron rod, about 6 inches long, carved with patterns that resemble flames emanating from one side. 
### Chamber 6
#### Hallway
> Inside the secret door, is a 20 foot long hallway that ends in a stone wall with an intricate carving of an open-mouthed dragon. Just inside the door is a 5 foot wide, 20 foot deep pit with a skeleton at the bottom, clutching a bag in one hand.

**Pit Trap:** The 5x5 area immediately after the tripped pit trap is *another* 20 foot deep pit trap.

**Stone Door:** Closer investigation of the carving reveals small hole, about an inch and a half across in the center of the dragon's open mouth. Inserting the rod from [[#Chamber 5]] into this hole will open the pressure plate doors and the door into the [[#Central Chamber]] (looking like a knob in a hobbit door) as long as the rod remains in the hole. 

**Treasure:** The skeleton's bag at the bottom of the pit contains 10 gp and a Potion of Healing.

#### Central Chamber
> The door grinds open to reveal a chamber with a massive vaulted ceiling. The figures on the walls in this hall are laying prostrate pointing towards the center of the room where an oxidized statue of a dragon sits. Gears, belts, and chains can be seen behind the rusted metal plating. Its metallic wings, extended. It's claws, raised to either side - as if in benediction. In it's jaws is a metal orb with detailed gearwork. It's blank eyes stare, unseeing, at the open door.

When looking at the statue from behind, characters with a passive Perception of 15+ notice a keyhole between the wings on its back. Inserting the key from [[#Chamber 4]] will disable the dragon, causing it to drop the Gearwork Orb

**Enemies:** The statue in the center of the room (the puddle space on the map) is a [[Rusted Clockwork Dragon Statue]] that animates when the Gearwork Orb in its mouth is removed. It can only rotate 180 degrees, so it cannot attack creatures between it and the back wall.
> As you withdraw the orb from its steel jaws, a shrill whistle blows. Its wings fold in and it lets loose a scream like tearing sheet metal. A portcullis drops from the ceiling, blocking the entrance, in response to its terrible screech.

# The Gearwork Orb
The orb from the mouth of the dragon statue in the [[#Central Chamber]] can be opened with the key from [[#Chamber 4]]. When the key is inserted...
> The outer shell of the orb falls away, revealing a knot of interlocking gears that can be turned to rotate patterned panels on different sides - like a Rubik's cube with curves and lines instead of solid colors. 
> 
> **D:** *"I'm not exactly sure what that is, but it's fascinating!"*

The following can be gleaned from the orb:
* Intelligence (Arcana) 10 reveals that there is an arcane mechanism inside that's awaiting for the correct configuration.
* Intelligence (Religion) 13 reveals that the faces can be configured to show the symbol of one of the following gods:
	* Faith
	* Hope
	* Love
	* Ambition
	* Impulse
	* Defiance
* Intelligence (Investigation) 12 can be used to rotate the orb to show a symbol of your choice.

When the orb has been set to Ambition...
> *\[\[HOLDER OF THE ORB\]\]* crumples to the floor, dropping the orb. Rolling away, it springs open and a flash of light fills the room. Hovering in midair over the orb is a blue projection of a familiar canyon amidst a jungle. The projection jerks, zooms out, and pans East to show a wide, sandy expanse. Pinpricks of blue light dot the sands and elongate into beams of light that reach to the sky.
> 
> **D:** *"Now that is just fantastic!"* 
> 
> He reaches towards the projection, his hand passing through it. The hologram flicks off, returning the room to its relative darkness.
> 
> **D:** *"Bring that back! Helena!"*
> 
> He turns to see her on the floor, kneeling over *\[\[HOLDER OF THE ORB\]\]* .
> 
> **H:** *"This wasn't supposed to happen..."*
