# Preamble
The party has been assembled to escort and attend to a supply caravan headed to the frontier settlement of [[Sand's End]] from the less rugged and more populous [[Hope's Embrace]].

On the day of departure, characters will arrive one at a time, giving everyone a chance to introduce themselves. When the first character arrives, read the following:
>As you approach the previously designated rallying point, you're greeted by a small, elderly halfling man with a bulbous and crooked back, arched over a cane. It's [[Matthias Grubbinski|Grubbinski]], the man that hired you for this little protection gig. 
>
>**G:** *"Oh yes, eeh hello. It's aaah fine day for a setoff, a fine day indeed. And how are you on this most fine morning? Doing well, I should hope?"*

Once all characters have arrived...
>**G:** *"And here come my [[Matthias Grubbinski#Family|brood]]. Friends, meet Pauline - my eldest's youngest; Mikul - my eldest's eldest; Gopher - my eldest's eldest's second son; and finally... Buster... my youngest son. Any questions?"*

After that...
> **G:** *"Ah heck! I almost forgot Reggie. Reggie!"*
> 
> Stepping out from around the cart is a small goblin with huge floppy ears and a large hat wearing billowy pantaloons and a necklace with a pendant.
> 
> **R:** *"Good evening, gentlemen. My name is Reginald VonSchmidtsly. At your service."* (he bows deeply) *"Sir, would you like me to powder your nose?"*
> 
> **G:** *"No! Quit asking me that! I picked up ol' Reggie here after I found him huddled in a bush somewhere up North wearing nothing but a loincloth shivering in the cold. He's been my traveling suitcase ever since."*
> 
> **R:** *"M'lord, I would be most appreciative if you stopped telling everyone you met that most dreadful tale about m-"*
> 
> **G:** *"Nah, it builds character. Let's be off! We're burning daylight!"*

During the overland journey, give the players time for their characters to introduce themselves to each other and talk with [[Matthias Grubbinski#Family|Grubbinski's Family]].
# Highway Robbery
> As your journey draws on, you can't help but feel like Grubbinski wasted money hiring you... and maybe you've wasted time getting hired by him.
> The road rounds a corner and is promptly blocked by a fallen tree.
> 
> **G:** *"Well string me up and call me Margret Thatcher. Good thing I brought some strapping young gents like yerselves along. Now giddy yap and get this thing outta here so we can make it to Sand's End before nightfall - I am just scraped to the britches of campin' out in this boonswaggle of a jungle."*

As the party begins to clear the blockage...
> **G:** *"If Buster didn't spend so long wipin' his ass, we would've been tuh town yesteryear. But as it is, I suppose I can't throw too much of a fit, we did - after all - make it here without incid-"*
> 
> Before he can finish his rambling thought, a man in a wide cattleman hat saunters out of of the wood, spurs jingling. 
> 
> **B1:** *"My my my, what do we have here"*
> 
> **G:** *"Aw heck. I need to quit flappin' my gums. I jinxed us!"*

Let the party jump in to potentially negotiate. If negotiations fail, the party is attacked by 5 **[[Highwayman]]** - the one talking and an additional 4 from the forest alongside the road, flanking the party.

Once the highwaymen have been dealt with...
> **R:** *"Would m'lord like me to powder his nose?"*
> 
> **G:** *"No! I told you to quit asking me that! Let's get back on the road - hopefully we can make Sand's End by nightfall."*

# Arrival In Town
> Your near the edge of the forest, a palisade appears before you. The tall logs obstruct what would otherwise be a wide open view of the sands beyond. Two men stand to atop the wall looking out at you from above the gate.

Once the party reaches the gate..
> **Guard:** *"Halt! Who goes there?"*
> 
> **G:** *"[[Sand's End#Guards|Harold]], you buffoon, it's me! Grubbinksi! You should have received word of our coming!"*
> 
> **H:** *"My bad, sir. We expected you days ago. We thought you'd fallen prey to the highwaymen."*
> 
> **G:** (under his breath) *"Not as if they didn't try."* (louder) *"Are ye going to let us in?"*
> 
> **H:** *"Of course, Grubbinski, sir. Um... just one small thing... the lord has instituted a small... uh... import tax on all goods."*
> 
> **G:** *"Why the blazes has he done that? It's not like there's folk lining up to come here!"*
> 
> **H:** *"This really is no topic to be yelling over the town walls about, come in."* (Opening the gate) *"Well, with the rise in highwaymen, he seeks to put together a force to protect the road."*
> 
> **G:** *"Bah! I paid for my own guard and now I have to pay for the next yokel's too?"*
> 
> **R:** *"Sir, would you care for me to powder your nose?"*
> 
> **G:** *"NO,  Reginald!! Fine, Harold. I'll pay his lordship's stupid tax."* (turning to the party) *"You run along now. I appreciate your help, but my family will help me set up shop."*

At this point, the party is free to visit whatever places in town they want.
