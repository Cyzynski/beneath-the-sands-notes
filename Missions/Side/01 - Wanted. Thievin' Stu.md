[[Sheriff Randall Boggs]] has been under a lot of pressure recently to eliminate the bandit threat harrying the mining expedition. He will *gladly* shell out most of his own salary just to keep his job.

He's put out a bounty for one "Thievin' Stu", granted it's a paltry one - since the man isn't particularly dangerous.

# The Job
This is what Sheriff Boggs has to share regarding Stu:
* His crew has burgled several carriages passing down the road to Sand's End. 
* They never kill anyone (not that the sheriff knows of at least), just threaten and then steal
* They reportedly cornered and robbed the postman on his way back to civilization.
	* The Sheriff only found out about this robbery once the next postman came back to town, reporting the theft. The original runner no longer handles the route.
* Like the poster says, there is a **25 GP** reward for bringing Stu to the sheriff alive - he can be harmed, but as long as he's still "suckin' wind" there will be a reward.
