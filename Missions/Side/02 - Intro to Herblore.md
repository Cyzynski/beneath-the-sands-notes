[[Persistent Sariel]] needs some herbs to create the medicine she needs to craft the remedy she needs to heal Rex.

>

>

# First Contact
Entering the small side room of the church...
>A elven woman in the flowing robes of the Aspect Priesthood stands over an injured Rex. 
>
>**S:** *"Oh good! You're here! Are you familiar with Makam and Knobweed?"*

The plants are needed to create additional [[Fortified Potion of Healing]]. Sariel is almost out of stock and the potion has to distill before use for maximum potency.

Makam is a type of tree that's not particularly uncommon in the area - identifiable by its round yet fern-like leaves. It's sap is a sticky ochre color and has a strong, musky odor. 
* Each Makam tree found has enough sap to fill 1 vial
* While looking in the woods for the trees, each hour, there is a chance that a {CREATURE OF SOME KIND} will attack them.

Knobweed is a reed-like plant about the length of a forearm with a large frond on top that curls up inside the reed during the day to not dry out. It grows in the rocky soil of the desert. Some can be found in {HEX NUMBER}.
* In a reasonably-sized field of Knobweed reeds, it takes 1 person a half hour to gather 1 vial of sap.
* While gathering Knobweed, the party may encounter [[Slither Spine]]s.

If the party can get her the materials needed, she will teach them how to make the medicine themselves. If they bring extra supplies, she'll make one or two extra for them.

Creating the potions requires an alchemists kit, the ingredients, and a week to "cook".
