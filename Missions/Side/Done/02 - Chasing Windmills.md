# The Farm
Once the party arrives:
>You round a hill and can now see, just ahead, that the road winds back and forth up a small hill. On top sits a barn and two windmills, separated by several fields in desperate need of a weeding.
>
>**Q:** *"This is it! The home stretch! To me, my cavaliers!"*
>
>He takes off, full tilt, up the hill, letting loose a war cry - during which his voice cracks at least once.

Donny will run forward and attack a windmill, eventually he may move on to attack the other, at which point it shows itself as a [[Quixote Chull]] and attacks!

Donny uses the Warrior Veteran stat block with the following changes:
* He has 50 HP
* He loses ***Multiattack***.
* He has a ***Hand Cannon*** instead of ***Heavy Crossbow****.

***Hand Cannon.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +3, range 30/90 ft., *Hit*: 8 (2d6 + 1) piercing damage.

Once defeated, Donald will try to convince them to cut up the beast for it's heart. (*"A beastie like this has to have a ticker worth at least 2 pocket watches!"*). Harvesting the *Gemheart* will take a while and require Arcana checks to remove it perfectly. The raw gem can be worth up to 100 GP, but it can be cut and refined into 3 50 GP emeralds.
