[[Sheriff Randall Boggs]] has been under a lot of pressure recently to eliminate the bandit threat harrying the mining expedition. He will *gladly* shell out most of his own salary just to keep his job.

He's put out a bounty for one "Thievin' Stu", granted it's a paltry one - since the man isn't particularly dangerous.

# The Job
This is what Sheriff Boggs has to share regarding Stu:
* His crew has burgled several carriages passing down the road to Sand's End. 
* They never kill anyone (not that the sheriff knows of at least), just threaten and then steal
* They reportedly cornered and robbed the postman on his way back to civilization.
	* The Sheriff only found out about this robbery once the next postman came back to town, reporting the theft. 
* Like the poster says, there is a **5 GP** reward for bringing Stu to the sheriff alive - he can be harmed, but as long as he's still "suckin' wind" there will be a reward.
	* Sheriff wants him alive so they can question him about and then reclaim the lost mail as well as any of the goods purloined from the carriages.
	* Plus, let's be honest, a few counts of armed robbery don't warrant letting him swing.
# The Hunt
The party can pick up Stu's gang's trail near where they were ambushed during their initial journey to Sand's End (this is the group that did it). They can be tracked down with 3 successful Wisdom (Survival) before 2 failures. If the party fails 2 before succeeding on 3, Stu and his cronies find *them* and ambush them in the woods. 

The trip from Sand's End to the ambush site takes 3 hours on foot, then each Survival check takes an additional 30 minutes.
# Stu's Hideout
Stu uses two [[Highwayman]] stat blocks smushed together and gets two turns in initiative - he's a slippery fella. With him are 6 additional [[Highwayman|Highwaymen]]. 

Once Stu has been apprehended:
>You notice a strange tattoo of a small sun on the back of his neck.

Stu is carrying double loot for a standard highwayman as well as one [[Vial of Embers]].

Around the hideout in various crates and the like, the following can be found
* Many pieces of opened mail including the following:
	* A letter addressed to Dr. Doppler (in an intricate cursive hand from his father, expressing concern about the lack of findings and anger at the wasted funds)
	* A letter addressed to Noonan (in a cramped and pointy handwriting from an unnamed person, talking about how he's gotta pay up soon "or else")
	* A letter addressed to Persistent Sariel (in tidy handwriting, from a "Timone" about how he misses her)
	* A letter addressed to Sheriff Boggs (in a chicken-scratch sort of handwriting from Grubbinski detailing the plans of the convoy)
* A fine pocket watch (10 GP)
* A silver locket containing picture of a bearded dwarf woman (2 GP)
* A [[Wand of Create Wand]] in a box with aggressive signage that the box is not to be opened.
* 120 SP *(not returned, since it wasn't the "stolen goods")*
* 200 CP *(not returned, since it wasn't the "stolen goods")*
# Resolution
The party killed one member of Stu's gang and then he gave himself up to protect the rest of his gang. Joseph and Betty went back to the gang, lied through their teeth, and were able to leave with (most of) the loot under the guise that they would return with Stu.

On the way back, the highwaymen - thinking something was up - sent riders back after the party to get either their stuff or Stu back. Unfortunately for them, Betty and Twinner had no qualms engaging in horse violence. The bandits were dispatched, Simon took the surviving horse, and Joseph patched up the living bandit just enough so he can hobble back.

After that, Betty and Twinner snooped through the letters recovered from the camp. Notably, no one ever opened the locked box.

They took Stu back to Sand's End, Twinner - after noticing Stu's tattoo - snuck over to the sheriff's office and set his bed on fire to put the fear of the gods into the criminal. His secret wasn't entirely without notice though, as Joseph noticed him sneaking back into his room in the tavern.
