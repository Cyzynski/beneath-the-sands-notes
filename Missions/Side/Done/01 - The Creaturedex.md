[[Miss K (Kavgravakk)]] wants to teach her students about some of the creatures in the area, but she doesn't have any textbooks on the matter. She would like to hire the party to document the creatures they come across. 

# Flier
> I need some research collected about local wildlife.
> Come by the school after class for more information.
> ~ Miss K

# First Contact
When the party arrives, if school is in session:
> You open the door onto a surprisingly placid scene. A tall, half-Orc woman stands at the front of a room of desks, all full of children of various ages. She's in the middle of reading aloud to the class, but trails off when you enter.
> 
> She slowly closes the book and looks up at you, her mouth a flat line, with a look that says "I'm not mad, just disappointed."
> 
> "Can I help you?"

If it's after hours:
> You open the door and see a tall, half-Orc woman tidying up a small one-room classroom. As you enter, she looks up at you with a smile.
> 
> "Can I help you boys?"
# The Job
> "The young'uns are so curious about all the varmints and critters out around here, but I just don't have the time. Could you gather up some information about them for me? Can any of y'all draw? That could make a nice addition."
>
> "I'll pay ya a little something for each one. The more information you can get me for each critter, the more I'll pay. Wha'd'ya say?"

**If the party raises a stink:**
> "Okay, big boy. I'm operating on a teacher's salary! I figured you'd do this for the love of the game... unless of course, you're all hat and no cattle - like my Ex husband."

Miss K will pay up to 2 GP for each entry. Less if less information is given.
# Resolution
The party has made one entry so far - for the giant boar, but they haven't turned it in yet.
