[[Willem Dasmith]] posted this flier to draw in someone - anyone - that will help him get back his favorite smithing apron from his ex-wife. Everything about the poster was to keep a local from taking up the task, and ensure that whoever did help knows nothing about his relationship with [[Miss K (Kavgravakk)]].

# Flier
> Brave Adventurers Needed for Asset Retrieval!
> Must be willing to enter the den of and confront hostile and toothy monsters!
> Negotiable Fee.
> See me at Iron Will for more information.
> ~ Willem

# First Contact
> The sound of a hammer striking an anvil greets you from inside. When you enter, the man keeps hammering away on what looks to be a pickaxe.
> 
> Unaware of your presence, he continues hammering away.

If the party interrupts:
> He holds up a hand, forestalling any further conversation. Then, using a pair of tongs, he places the piece he was working on back into the coals of his forge.

If sufficient time passes:
> Using a pair of tongs, he dunks the piece in the quench with a hiss, causing steam to fill the air. He draws the piece out, looks at it, shrugs slightly, and then sets it on the workbench. 
> 
> Finally noticing you and...

After the initial:
> Wiping his greasy hands on his tattered and threadbare apron, he approaches you.
> 
> *"Good {morning/afternoon}. You folks must be new to Sand's End. Hopefully y'all are here for something more interesting than mining tools."*

# The Job
Once the flier has been brought up:
> With a sheepish grin, he scratches his head.
> 
> *"I'm afraid my notice was a bit of a misdirect - I didn't want any locals taking up my request, they might be a little too close to the situation."*
> 
> *"The asset I need you to retrieve is my favorite apron."* 
> 
> (he gestures at the torn up one he's currently wearing) 
> 
> *"This rag just doesn't compare.... and the monster you might come across isn't so much a monster as she is an old... eh... acquaintance. Have you met Kavgravakk?"*

The following is what Willem will share:
* He accidentally left the apron over at K's one night after work, and then things ended between them before he could get it back.
* He'd get it back himself, but he thinks it best to keep his distance - he doesn't want to upset her.
	* He will **NOT** share why things ended between the two of them. Especially not to some people he's just met
* He is willing to pay 5 gold for it and will throw in any blade he has in shop.
	* If the party wants more than that: *"As much as I love that apron and the memories I have with it, I paid 3 coper for it - It'd be silly of me to pay more than what I've offered."*
