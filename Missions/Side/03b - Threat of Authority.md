The party learns a corrupt noble in a nearby town in the foothills of the mountains is using a magical bracelet to control a **[Stone Giant](https://www.aidedd.org/monster/stone-giant)** and impose his will on the local townsfolk. A refugee offers to hire the party to steal the bracelet.
# Preamble
[[Persistent Sariel]], some time after the party helped her replenish her potion stores (as part of [[02 - Intro to Herblore]]), will approach the party to help refugees that have sought shelter at the chapel.
* The refugees are from [[Exemplar]]
	* Their leader is named [[Albric Townsend]].
* [[Mayor Blaise Dauterive]] recently came into possession of an enchanted bracelet that can be used to control the Stone Giant, **Hogar**.
	* He is also [[Simon - Brandon]]'s cousin.
	* He forces Hogar to trample fields or uses him as a treat to keep the townsfolk in line.
* Albric's home was recently crushed to "make an example of him" after his mining crew failed to meet their quota.
	* His daughter, **Maelle** was home at the time (unbeknownst to <MAYOR_NAME>) and was killed.
	* Fed up with their treatment, but powerless to stop it, Albric packed up and fled in the night with his crew.
* Sariel would like the party to steal or destroy the bracelet. 
	* Albric would also be happy with the killing of the giant - an act that Sariel finds detestable.
	* Albric also suggests that the party could collect payment for themselves from the mayor's estate - Sariel has no opinion on this.
	* The party should be warned that they'll have to contest with the entirety of the town guard.
# Exemplar
*The party can case the city before moving on the mayor. It's much larger than Sand's End, there are side streets galore as well as a sewer system.*

As the party nears the city, they see the giant, either walking around the town or laying behind the mayor's estate, asleep.
