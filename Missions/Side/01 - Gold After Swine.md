[[Woodsman Troy]] recently lost a dog on a hunt when a monstrously large boar tore through the woods and gored the poor thing. Troy would like other to believe that he took a few shots at the beast before fleeing and deftly outwitted the beast, but he just scrambled up the nearest tree to wait for the boar to move on.

# First Contact
When the party arrives
> You approach the "Fresh Meat" guild building, just north of the Dirt Gate, slightly nestled into the wood. 
> You hear clucking from around the building before a squawk squawk, cut off by a thud.
> 
> *"Ew-ew-ew! Silas! Finish up back here, I got some bird in my eye!"*
> 
> A man in a wide-brimmed hat and duster steps around the corner, shaking his blood-covered gloves as he disgustedly yanks them off before wiping some bright liquid off of his face. He registers your presence and immediately straightens his shoulders. 
> 
> *"He-he, I'm just yankin' your chain, pointy-ears. You probably wouldn't even do it right - as goose-fingered as you are."* He makes a show of noticing you, as if for the first time.
> 
> *"Well howdy, what can I do ya for?"*

# The Job
The following is what Troy will share (or not share if he can help it):
* The massive boar gored his dog on a hunt a tenday back
	* Troy got away by outwitting the beast after *"laying a few in his flank."* (He really ran, squealing much like the hog and scrambled up the tree)
	* *"It ate the poor thing's collar!"* (the party may question how he saw this if he had run away, this should lead them to realize he's not being totally honest with the situation. He saw this from up in the tree before the boar wandered off)
* It's twice the size of most of the boars in the area and has barbed extra tusks to match.
* It was last seen in the woods North East of Sand's End, about half a day's ride.
* The guild will pay 50 GP for the head of the beast.

After conversation begins to wrap up:
> *"So can I count on you to beef the monster? I'd gladly tag along, but y'all seem to be a capable lot."*

If the party tries to bring him along, he will protest, but will acquiesce if party seem to think him cowardly.

Before parting:
> *"Now don't go squatting with your spurs on, partners. The beast is cunning and powerful for sure."*

# The Hunt
The party must make survival checks to track the beast, successful checks will result in them noticing things like:
* Hoofprints
* Trampled grass & snapped twigs
* Places where the boar has rubbed up against the tree
* Droppings

The must get 3 successes before 2 failures. If they fail, the boar will surprise them, charging out of the woods. If they succeed, they find the boar. If they sneak up on the beast, they surprise it.

The **Dire Boar** acts as two separate [https://www.aidedd.org/monster/giant-boar|Giant Boars] in initiative (taking two turns per round). It has the hit points of 2.5 Giant Boars.
