# The Church of the Aspects
The dominant religion across the continent is the Church of the Aspects. Followers believe that by praying to one of the Aspects, they can strengthen it in both themselves and the world. Each of the gods has an avatar on the material plane that they use to enact their plans, or interact with (or perhaps to subjugate) mortals.

The Aspects themselves were believed to have once been one in Creation. That is, until [[The Splintering]].

Followers of the church usually choose one Aspect to follow above the others, making them "Primary Devotees" of that Aspect. All priests of the church, even though they encourage devotion to any of the Divine Aspects, do primarily devote themselves to just one. This is why a priest's title reflects their Primary Aspect (i.e. Ambitious, Defiant, Faithful, Hopeful, Impulsive, Persistent, Passionate).
## Ambition
It is believed that Ambition is the only Aspect that was not part of the original number and that he reached out and seized godhood for himself.

In legend, he was worshipped by the [[Zmoka]] people until they decided to "break free" of him and slayed him. Because of this, there are very few *Ambitious*. They credit this lack of devotion to why the modern world holds few wonders compared to the legends of the fabled Zmoka.

## Faith
The *Faithful* are sometimes (respectfully) looked down on by other members of the church for choosing to follow an aspect that's main goal is increasing the number of the faithful, and not enacting any real change in the world.
## Hope
The most popular Aspect of the pantheon, because who doesn't want to live in a more hopeful world? 

The most common criticism of the *Hopeful* is that "Hope and half a silver will get you an ale". The *Hopeful* generally refute this by saying that any one aspect on it's own is powerless to positively influence the world, but that without Hope, all life would be misery.

# Providence
Providence is the goddess of fate and destiny. The followers of Providence fall into two main camps:
1. Those that believe that their duty is to find their destiny through a devoted life of prayer. Then, once they've found it, follow it to the best of their ability in honor of Providence.
2. Those that think their destiny will find them regardless of what they do - these followers tend to be largely indistinguishable from people that don't follow any particular deity. It's more of a token belief than anything else.
