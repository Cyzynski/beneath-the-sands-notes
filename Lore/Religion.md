# The Church of the Aspects
The dominant religion across the continent is the Church of the Aspects. Followers believe that by praying to one of the aspects, they can strengthen it in both themselves and the world. Each of the gods has an avatar on the material plane that they use to enact their plans, or interact with (or perhaps to subjugate) mortals.
## Ambition
Was worshipped by the [[Zmoka]] people until they decided to "break free" of him and slayed him to seize their destinies for themselves.
