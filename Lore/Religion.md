# The Church of the Aspects
The dominant religion across the continent is the Church of the Aspects. Followers believe that by praying to one of the Aspects, they can strengthen it in both themselves and the world. Each of the gods has an avatar on the material plane that they use to enact their plans, or interact with (or perhaps to subjugate) mortals.

The Aspects themselves were believed to have once been one. That is, until the Splintering.

## Ambition
It is believed that Ambition is the only Aspect that was not part of the original number and that he reached out and seized godhood for himself.

He was worshipped by the [[Zmoka]] people until they decided to "break free" of him and slayed him to seize their destinies for themselves.
