In the beginning, there was Creation alone, in the Nothing. He felt alone in the desolate and empty to him. He created a small pocket in the Nothing to call his home. This was not enough. He created another pocket in the infinite Nothing and, with it, brought into existence the cosmos - all the stars, planets, and moons. This was not enough. He breathed life into the planets, separating the land from the sea. He created forests and streams. He molded the animals from the earth, creating each and every one by hand. This was not enough. He desired more, and most importantly, others to share that with. 

From this desire, he crafted his children - lesser deities - to govern the forces of the world. Flora, to tend the wilds; Guile, to inspire creativity; {OTHERS HERE}; and finally, Providence, to oversee fate. This was finally enough, and he rested to observe all his creations working together. The beautiful tapestry fascinated him, and was all the more beautiful for it was not made by him alone, but by his family - working together and sharing their gifts. His children and their creations were not perfect, but it was precisely because of these imperfections that he was able to finally feel he had done enough.

This was, however, not enough for Creation's children. They resented that there was a being greater than them. They conspired against him, and - led by Guild - sought to kill him. Creation, of course, knew this was coming. The gifts he bestowed on his children, he also possessed. This allowed him to foresee his children's trickery through the tides of fate.

When they finally moved against Creation, at the moment of his death, he instead chose to splinter himself into 6 Divine Aspects, and leave behind sentient life to embody those Aspects. It was this final act that cemented in the minds of his children that what they had done was wrong, but could not be undone. That is why they make no moves against the folk of the realms, but work with them - cherishing (and in some cases craving) the devotion they give.
