#NPC/Minor

A small goblin and self-proclaimed manservant of [[Matthias Grubbinski]]. He is vey "posh" and "proper".

His true identity is that of a most uncivilized Goblin, by the name of Cupcake, who was "blessed" (if you could call it that) with sentience and an advanced sense of culture. The source of this "boon" (again, if you could all it that) is the pendant he wears about his neck - removing that would lead to a truly embarrassing moment for Reginald and the freedom of a salivating Cupcake.
