The Lizardfolk Randal is the law in town (which, in town is fairly unnecessary. The folk of Sand's End are a peaceful bunch - his main adversaries are the bandits and brigands that plague both the road into town from civilization as well as the miners and adventurers in the deep desert). He is assisted by his deputies/town guard, but all power in town ultimately falls to him.
