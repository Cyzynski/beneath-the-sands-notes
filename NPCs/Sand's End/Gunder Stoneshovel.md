A stout dwarven man with a fondness for drink and an even greater fondness for gambling that leads [[Dust Lake Mining Company|the company]]. He views every expedition as one big bet against the house. He gets paid a paltry sum directly by the lord of Sand's End, with promises of much greater bonuses when ancient treasures are found.

Also a chain-smoker.
