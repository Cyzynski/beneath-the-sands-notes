Grove University is the most prestigious (and only) school in the Northern Expanse. It is located in [[Leafall]] and is overseen by The Board of Regents - a faceless group that makes all decisions regarding admissions. 
## Courses
Since the university is the only of its kind, well anywhere nearby, it's sprawling campus offers classes on all manner of topics from agriculture to wizardry, from music to archaeology.
## Notable NPCs
- [[Dr. Malcom Doppler]], professor of Archaeology
- [[Helena Cartier]], prospective grad student studying ancient magic
