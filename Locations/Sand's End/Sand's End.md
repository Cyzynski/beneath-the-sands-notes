Located on the brink of the arcane sands of the deep desert is the frontier settlement of Sand's End.
## Places of Interest
* Dust Gate - the Western gate that leads out into the wastes.
* Dirt Gate - the Northern gate that leads into the woods and towards civilization
* [[The Chapel]] - the only religious institution in town, located nearest to the Dirt Gate.
* [[Notice Board]] - standing in the shade of the central tree is a large board on which the townsfolk put up requests and notices.
* [[School of Rocks]] - where all the children of Sand's End are taught, to the best of the lone teacher's abilities, located on the southern side of the central plaza.
* [[Sheriff's Office]] - located across the thoroughfare from the Chapel by the Dirt Gate.
### Businesses
* [[General's Store]] - for all your adventuring needs! Located near Dirt Gate
* [[Iron Will]] - a small blacksmith shop in the Northeast corner of the center of town.
* [[Lockwood Flints]] - the local gunsmith, located just clockwise of Iron Will.
* [[Sand's End Tavern]] - located on Northwestern half of the central circle of town.
* [[Dust Lake Mining Company]] - Housed in the five buildings closest to the Dust Gate. Of those 5 buildings, the one closest to town is their main office and bunkhouse for the head honchos. The four remaining buildings are used for storing a combination of the men and their tools.
* [[Fresh Meat]] - a group of hunters and woodsmen that bring fresh meat into town. They also tend to serve as impromptu additional guards if need be.
