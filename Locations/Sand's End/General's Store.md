New in town! Owned and operated by [[Matthias Grubbinski]], located near the [[Sand's End]]'s Dirt Gate.

## Shop Features
* ***Haggling.*** Grubbinski loves a good bartering session he's all about "cuttin' deals and spinnin' wheels" he's willing to offer an up to 10% discount if properly incentivized.
* ***Guarantee.*** He sources his wares from all over... they don't always work exactly as intended. Whenever a player buys something magic, roll a d20, on a 1, the item isn't exactly what it seems.

## Stock
Some items take time to ship in, whatever is narratively convenient and keeps the shop interesting. Whenever new stock arrives, roll on the following indicated tables, the shown number of times.
