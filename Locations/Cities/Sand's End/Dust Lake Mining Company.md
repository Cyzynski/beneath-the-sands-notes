The outlet that employs all those who work excavating in the dunes of the deep desert - which at this point in time is most of the fair hamlet of [[Sand's End]]. The buildings the company owns in town are used to store extra equipment and employees not on expedition.

Owned and operated by [[Gunder Stoneshovel]].
