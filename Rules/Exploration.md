*(Adapted from Survivalists Guide to Spelunking)*

Each day, while exploring, the party can take **three** actions:
* **Travel.** Moving from one hex into the next. When traveling over roads, two hexes can be traversed with a single action. Some hexes may require additional actions to cross. Traveling off road can also lead to the party getting lost.
* **Explore.** Looking for interesting features or something specific in a hex. Depending on the terrain, a Survival or Nature check may be required.
	* **Retracing the Path.** Looking for something specific that was previously encountered in a hex.
* **Camp.** Resting and sleeping. 
	* If the party does not take the camp action for the day, they can still sleep, but they all take the [[Survival#Resting Penalties|resting penalty]] associated with not finding a campsite.
*  **Forage.** Hunting or foraging for food or plants.
