*Wonderous item, rare (requires attunement)*
*(From dndbeyond content drops)*

---

The amulet has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. When you restore Hit Points to one other creature, you can expend 1 charge to regain the same amount of Hit Points.
