*Wonderous item, Uncommon (requires attunement)*

---

Once per day, as a Magic action, you point at another creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself and focus divine energy at it. Roll 1d8 and add your Wisdom modifier. Choose one:
* You restore Hit Points to the creature equal to that total .
* You force the creature to make a *Constitution saving throw*: DC: 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier. 
	* *Failure*: the creature takes Necrotic or Radiant damage (your choice) equal to that total. 
	* *Success*: half damage.
