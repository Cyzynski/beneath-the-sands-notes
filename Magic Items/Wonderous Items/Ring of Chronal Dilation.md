*Wonderous item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

---

This ring looks to be made of flowing sand.

***Chronal Dilation.*** As a Magic Action, you can take 1d4 Necrotic damage to store your chronal energy in the ring. During your next turn, you gain an additional Action.
