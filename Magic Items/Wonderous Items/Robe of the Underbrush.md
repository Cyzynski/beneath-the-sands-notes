*Wonderous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Druid or Ranger)*
*(From Griffin's Saddlebag)*

---

This robe feels like a large, soft leaf. While wearing it, any evidence of your travel is magically hidden.

***Magical Hiding.*** Other creatures take a -5 penalty to any check made to track you or those traveling with you.

***Hardened Cloak.*** Once per day, you can use a Bonus Action to case *Barkskin* on yourself from the cloak without spending a spell slot.
