*Wonderous item, common*
*(From @me.mimic)*

---

***Light.*** The vial sheds dim light in a 5-foot radius.

***Spark.*** As an Interact action, you can open the vial to ignite flammable objects within a 5-foot cube within 5 feet of you. Once you use this ability, the vial looses its magic until the next dawn.

***Warmth.*** The vial radiates a steady, comfortable warmth of about 95 degrees, regardless of the surrounding temperature.
