*Shield, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)*
*(From @thedungeonlab)*

---

The shield has a number of charges equal to your Strength modifier (minimum of 1). It regains all expended changes when you finish a Long Rest.

You can expend these charges in one of the following ways:
* **Stalwart Brace.** When an effect would push, pull or otherwise move you against your will, you can use your reaction to expend 1 charge and reduce the distance moved by 10ft.
* **Stalwart Shove.** As a bonus action, you can expend 1 charge to make a shove attack with advantage.

---
*(From official unarmed strike rules)*

**Shove.** The target must succeed on a *Strength* or *Dexterity* saving throw (it chooses which), or you either push it 5 feet away or cause it to have the Prone condition. The DC for the saving throw equals 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus. This shove is possible only if the target is no more than one size larger than you.
