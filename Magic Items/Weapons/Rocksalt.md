*Coach Gun, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Cleric or Paladin)*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magic ranged weapon is a coach gun caked with crystalline salt growths. 

***Consecrating Blast.*** Once per turn, instead of making an attack roll with this weapon, you can fire a cloud of sacred salt in a 15 foot Cone. Each Celestial, Fey, Fiend or Undead within the are of the Cone must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or gain a 1d4 penalty to attack rolls and ability checks and be unable to teleport or travel between planes for the next 10 minutes.
