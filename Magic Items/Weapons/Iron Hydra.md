*Volley Gun, Uncommon*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magic weapon is an oxide-encrusted firearm that is
seeping with benthic magic. The copper-colored scales running
along the barrel shift up and down, and the eyes at the end of
the muzzle glow with a green hue.

***Duplicating Reload.*** Whenever this weapon is reloaded, it gains an additional barrel for one minute (up to six additional barrels). Each additional barrel adds an extra 2 (1d4) Acid damage to attacks made with this weapon, and increases the Save DC for the weapon’s Burst Fire property.
