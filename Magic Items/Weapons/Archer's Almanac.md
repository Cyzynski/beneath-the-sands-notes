*Wonderous Item (quiver), Uncommon (requires attunement)*
*(Adapted from @sinlaire_box)*

---

The almanac holds up to 20 bookmarks. While the book is closed, you can draw a bookmark from it as part of making a ranged weapon attack with a bow or crossbow. The bookmark becomes a piece of magical ammunition, vanishing into dust after attacking. 

During a rest, you can spend 10 minutes and one sheet of fine parchment and ink to add one bookmark to the book (up to its maximum of 20).

***Favored Quarry.*** When you finish a Long Rest, you can choose one creature type. Attacks you make with ammunition from the Almanac against creatures of the chosen type get a +1 bonus to the attack and damage rolls and deal an extra 1d6 damage of the weapon's damage type.
