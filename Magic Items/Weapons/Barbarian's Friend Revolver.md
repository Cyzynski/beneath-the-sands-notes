*Revolver, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Barbarian)*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This massive magical revolver is loaded with special, high-powered ammunition, which is infused with the primordial energy of deep earth metals. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

***Cover Breaker***. If an attack misses a target behind half-cover or higher, that piece of cover is destroyed and the target takes 1d6 Thunder damage.

***Raging Firearm***. You can make attacks and damage rolls with this weapon with your Strength modifier instead of Dexterity.
