*Palm Pistol, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Monk)*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magic ranged weapon is a silver plunger-fired palm pistol attached to the wielder’s wrist. Focal mantras are engraved onto its turret-like cylinder, which sports seven barrels. 

***Arcane Nature.*** This weapon has seven charges, and does not require ammunition. 

***Close Range.*** Attacks made with this weapon do not suffer disadvantage from having hostile creatures stand within 5 feet of you. 

***Focused Blast.*** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with this weapon you can expend one charge to deal extra Piercing damage to the target equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die. 

***Focus Powered.*** As a Magic Action while attuned to this item, you can expend Focus Points to replenish expended charges. The Chakravarti regains charges equal to the number of Focus points you expend as a part of this action, up to the maximum number of charges it can hold.
