*Musket, Uncommon*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magic ranged weapon is a gold-plated musket with an ashtray built into its flashpan. The burning aroma of cigarette smoke fills the air as you train your weapon on a target.

***Smokescreen.*** Whenever you make an attack with this weapon on your turn, you produce a line of magical smoke that engulfs the target on a hit. The target must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or become Blinded until the end of its next turn. The first time a creature fails its saving throw against this effect, they gain a level of exhaustion, which is removed whenever they succeed on the saving throw or after one minute. A creature that has suffered this Exhaustion cannot gain another level from this weapon for the next 24 hours.
