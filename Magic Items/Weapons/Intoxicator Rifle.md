*Rifle, Common*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magical rifle is constructed from alchemical bronze and contains an endless supply of distilled venom within a globe attached to its magazine. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

Whenever you hit a creature with an attack made using this rifle, the target must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 2d6 Poison damage and becoming Poisoned for the next 10 minutes on a failure, or taking half as much and not being poisoned on a success. While Poisoned this way, the target has Disadvantage on Strength, Constitution, and Dexterity ability checks.
