*Shotgun, Uncommon*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This aberrant shotgun is made from meteoric iron and the mineral deposits produced by the aberrant ice slugs that inhabit the meteors from which it is extracted.

Whenever you hit a creature with an attack made with this weapon, you can choose to deal either Acid or Cold damage with the attack A creature that takes Acid damage from this attack suffers a -1 penalty to their Armor Class until the start of its next turn, while a creature that takes Cold damage from the attack has its movement speed reduced by 10 feet for the next minute.
