*Revolver, Uncommon (+1),  Rare (+2), Very Rare (+3)*
*(Adapted from @thefantasticemporium)*

---

***Arcane Enhancement.*** You get a bonus to your attack and damage rolls made with this weapon equal to its rarity bonus (RB).

***Forged Fire.*** This weapon ignores the ammunition property. It still must be "reloaded" to vent heat. It shoots bolts of condensed fire, dealing Fire damage instead of the normal Piercing and ignites any flammable objects that aren't being worn or carried.

***Flamethrower.*** This weapon has a number of charges equal to twice its RB and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. Once per turn, when you take the attack action with this weapon, you can expend a charge and replace one of your attacks with a 15ft. cone of fire emanating from this weapon. All creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 11 + twice this item's RB). Taking damage equal to a number of rolls of this weapon's damage die equal to its RB on a failed save or half that on a success. Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried are ignited.
