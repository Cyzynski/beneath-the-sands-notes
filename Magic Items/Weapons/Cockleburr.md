*Coach Gun, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Druid or Ranger)*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magical wooden shotgun is grown directly from a desert cedar sapling and emanates green energy from its budding leaves. 

***Embedded Rounds.*** Whenever you hit a creature with an attack made with this weapon, they are infected with magical burrs that dig into their skin, forcing them to take 1d4 Magical Piercing damage for every 5 feet they move on their turn. The burrs last for one minute, or until another creature succeeds on a DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) check to remove them. Once the effect ends, the creature becomes immune to the magical burrs for one ten-day.
