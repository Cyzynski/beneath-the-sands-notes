*Carbine, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Ranger)*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magical carbine is constructed from white pine varnished with an oil extract of wolfsbane. Sylvan etchings along the weapon’s wooden furniture glow red when in the proximity of lycanthropes that match the depicted monster. 

***Attack Bonus.*** You gain a +1 to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, which doubles when attacking Lycanthropes and Shapeshifters.

***Wolfsense.*** Whenever a Lycanthrope or Shapeshifter approaches within 60 feet of you, the weapon glows to warn you. As long as you are not Blinded, you cannot be Surprised while the weapon glows.

***Silver Demise.*** Silver bullets fired through this weapon that hit Lycanthropes or Shapeshifters automatically Critical Hit.
