*Wonderous Item, Uncommon, requires Attunement*
*(Adapted from @thefantasticemporium)*

---

This magic ranged weapon is a long barreled, break-action hand cannon. It cannot be used like a normal firearm.

***Scroll Casting.*** As a Magic Action, you can load a spell scroll into this weapon instead of a bullet and cast it. In order to do so, the spell on that scroll must deal damage and have a casting time of 1 action. Scrolls used this way disappear after use.

## Rare Variant:
The rare variant of this weapon has all the features above, plus

***Arcane Sentience.*** The gun is sentient and remembers spells cast with it. As a Magic Action, (if the gun allows it) you can cast any Cantrip that has been cast through the gun since you attuned to it. When a new creature attunes to it, it loses all its memories.
