*Pistol, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Wizard)*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magical ranged weapon is a finely engraved, double barreled wheel-lock pistol inscribed with arcane sigils that glow with the faint orange light of transmutation magic. 

***Freeloader.*** This weapon ignores the Loading property.

***Arcane Readiness.*** While attuned to this weapon, you add your Intelligence modifier to your Initiative.
