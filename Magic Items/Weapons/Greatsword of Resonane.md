*Weapon (Greatsword), Uncommon (+1)
*(Inspired by @thedungeonlab)*

---

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic greatsword.

**Resonance.** When you roll the same number on both damage dice for an attack made with this weapon, the blade vibrates with harmonic energy. The attack deals an extra d6 Thunder damage.
