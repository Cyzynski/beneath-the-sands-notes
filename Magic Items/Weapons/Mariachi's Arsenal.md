*Carbine, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Bard)*
*(From Gunslingers Guide to Rexfald)*

---

This magical ranged weapon is a concealed rifle built into the neck of a guitar. Floral patterns carved into the wooden body hide the mechanism for this weapon, which can be operated seamlessly while performing. 

***Percussive Performance.*** Whenever you make an attack with this weapon on your turn, you can use one charge of your Bardic Inspiration to cause a blast of recoiling arcane energy that spreads from the point you’re standing in. Creatures of your choosing within 5ft must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be moved 10 feet away from you in a straight line.
