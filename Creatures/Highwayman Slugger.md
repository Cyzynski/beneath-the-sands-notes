## Abilities
**_Spread_**.  Once per turn, when the Highwayman hits a creature with a shotgun attack, it can make an additional ranged attack roll against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is also within range. On a hit, the second creature takes 5 (1d10) piercing damage.

## Actions
**_Multiattack_**. The Highwayman makes two Shotgun attacks.

**_Shotgun_**.  _Ranged Attack Roll_: +5, range 40/120 ft. _Hit_: 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage. The shotgun holds 5 rounds, after all are fired, the Highwayman must spend an Action or Bonus Action to reload.
