## Traits
***Hunter of the Living.*** The Imbued knows the location of all living (non-undead, non-construct) creatures within 200 feet of it.

***The Brink of Death.*** While bloodied, the Imbued gains an additional Legendary Action and an additional attack action as part of its **Multiattack**. The first time the Imbued becomes bloodied it immediately recharges its use of **Sand Echo**.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The imbued makes two **Cursed Blade** attacks as well as an additional **Cursed Blade** attack when bloodied. It can replace one of them with **Withering Sands** or **Sand Echo** if it is available.

***Cursed Blade.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. The Imbued gains temporary hit points equal to the amount of Necrotic damage dealt this way.

***Withering Sands (Recharge 5-6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw*: DC 15, each enemy in a 15-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the Imbued can see within 60 feet. *Failure*: 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. *Success*: Half damage only. *Success or Failure*: the Imbued gain temporary hit points equal to the total damage dealt.

***Sand Echo (1/Day).*** The imbued creates a duplicate of itself made of sand. The duplicate has AC 15, 1 HP, and can only make one **Cursed Blade** attack on each of its turns. The duplicate shares its initiative. 
## Bonus Actions
***Temporal Slip.*** The Imbued moves up to half of its movement without provoking opportunity attacks as a cloud of sand erupts from behind it.
## Reactions
***Sandy Riposte.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 15 feet of the Imbued misses the Imbued with an attack. *Response*: The Imbued moves up to 15ft. towards the triggering creature, without provoking opportunity attacks and leaving sand in its wake. Then, it makes a **Cursed Blade** attack against it if it is within range.
## Legendary Actions (2, 3 if Bloodied)
**Gaze of Death.** *Charisma Saving Throw*, one creature the Imbued can see within 60 ft. of it; DC 15. *Failure*. The creature is Frightened of the Imbued until the end of the Imbued's next turn.

***Cursed Blade.*** The Imbued makes a **Cursed Blade** attack against a creature within range.

***Temporal Slip.*** The Imbued uses **Temporal Slip**.
