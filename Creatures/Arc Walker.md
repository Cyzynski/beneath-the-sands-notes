## Traits
***Charging.*** Whenever the arc walker is subjugated to lightning damage, its ionize ability recharges. Additionally, it regains hit points equal to the amount of lightning damage it was subjected to.

***Bloodied - Damaged Grounding.*** While bloodied, the arc walker's lightning damage immunity turns into a resistance. Additionally, when a creature ends its turn within 5 feet of the arc walker, it takes 7 (2d6) lightning damage.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The arc walker makes two Arc Cannon attacks. When both attacks hit the same target, *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, *Failure*: The target becomes stunned until the end of its turn.

***Arc Cannon.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +6, range 60 ft. *Hit*: 22 (4d10) lightning damage.

***Arc Rail (Recharge 5/6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 14, each creature in a 60 ft long, 5 ft wide line. *Failure*: 28 (8d6) lightning damage. *Success*: Half damage.

***Spell Break Pulse (1/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw*: DC 14, each creature in a 30 ft. emanation originating from the arc walker. *Failure*: The target loses concentration on a spell and can't cast spells until the end of its next turn.
## Bonus Actions
***Ionize (Recharge 5-6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw*: DC 14, one creature within 60 feet of the arc walker that is can see. *Failure*: The target has disadvantage on Constitution Saving Throws and attacks that deal lightning damage have advantage against the target. This effect lasts until the end of the target's next turn.
