## Traits
***Herd Protection.*** The Bosinyx gains a +1 bonus to its AC for each allied Bosinyx within 5 feet of it.

***Siege Monster.*** The Bosinyx deals double damage to objects and structures.

***Stampede Instinct.*** When the Bosinyx is frightened, its movement speed increases by 10 feet. Additionally, it can still willingly move closer to the source of its fear.

## Actions
***Ram.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +7 (ADV if the Bosinyx moved 20+ feet straight toward the target immediately before), reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 23 (4d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. This attack scores a critical hit on a natural 19 and 20. On a critical hit, the target is knocked prone.

## Reactions

***Stick Together.*** *Trigger:* An allied creature within 5ft of the Bosinyx moves. *Response:* The Bosinyx moves along with that creature up to its speed. Each hostile creature within 5 feet of the Bosinyx as it moves it targeted by the following effect: *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 15. *Failure:* The target is knocked prone.
