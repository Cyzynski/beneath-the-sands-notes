*Tiny #Construct, Unaligned

---
**AC** 16
**HP** 26 (4d8 + 8)
**Initiative** +3 (13)
**Speed** 40ft., fly 40 ft.

**Immunities** poison, psychic, Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60ft, passive Perception 10
**Languages** understands Draconic and Zmokan, but can't speak.
**CR** 1 (PB +2)  #CR/2 
## Traits
***Bloodied - Overheating.*** When the dragon becomes bloodied for the first time, if it is Large or larger, it recharges its Rust Breath. While bloodied, it gains resistance to fire damage, but whenever it takes 5 or more fire damage, it gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.aidedd.org/en/rules/conditions/). All of the dragon's damaging effects deal an additional 1d6 fire damage while it is Overhearing.
## Actions
***Multiattack (Small or Larger).*** The dragon makes two Rend  attacks. It can replace one with a use of Scrap Barrier if it is available. It gains an extra use of Rend for each size category over Medium.

***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 7 (1d6 + 3) Slashing damage. This attack deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.

***Rust Breath (Large or Larger) (1/Day).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure*: 27 (6d8) acid damage and each metal object a target is wearing takes a -1 penalty to the AC it offers or its attack rolls. Armor is destroyed if its AC is reduced to 10 and weapons are destroyed if the penalty ever reaches -5. The penalty can be removed by casting the spell *mending* or an hour of repair with the appropriate tools per level of penalty. *Success*: Half damage only.

***Scrap Barrier (Medium or Larger) (Recharge 5-6).*** The dragon gains 2d6 temporary hit points, whenever a creature hits the dragon with a melee attack while it has any of those temporary hit points that duration, it takes 5 (2d4) slashing damage. This effect deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.
## Bonus Actions
***Overclock.*** The dragon can make one additional Rend attack as part of its Multiattack this turn. Then next turn, it makes one fewer Rend or Steel Scale attacks and it can't use this ability.

***Augment (Large or Smaller).*** The construct absorbs another Modular Clockwork Dragon within 5ft of it and its size increases by one category. It gains the hit points of the absorbed dragon. 

***Diminish (Medium or Larger).*** The construct ejects another Modular Clockwork Dragon to an unoccupied space within 5ft of it and its size decreases by one category. The taken damage is split between the dragons.
