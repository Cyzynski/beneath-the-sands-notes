## Traits
***Superior Magi Resistance.*** The spellguard has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. Spell attack rolls have Disadvantage against the spellguard.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The spellguard makes two attacks using Force Flail or Javelin in any combination

***Force Flail.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning plus 7 (2d6) Force damage.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll*: +7, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit*: 14 (3d6 + 4) Piercing damage.

## Reactions
***Defensive Divination.*** *Trigger*: A creature the spellguard can see hits an ally within 5 ft of the spellguard with an attack roll, or an ally within 5 ft of the spellguard fails a saving throw against a spell or other magical effect. *Response*: The spellguard rolls 1d20 and chooses whether to use that roll in place if the d20 rolled for the attack roll or saving throw.
