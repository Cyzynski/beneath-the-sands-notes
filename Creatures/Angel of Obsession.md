## Traits
**Magic Resistance.** The angel has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The angel makes two attacks, using Bite, Spear, or Radiant Ray in any combination.

***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 12 (2d6 + 5) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Radiant damage.

***Spear*.** *Melee Attack Roll*: +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit*: 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target is Large or smaller, the target is pushed up to 15 feet straight away from the angel.

***Radiant Ray.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +7, range 60 ft. *Hit*: 14 (3d6 + 4) Radiant damage and the target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

***Overwhelming Presence (Recharge 6).*** *Charisma Saving Throw*: DC 15, each enemy in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the angel can see within 120 feet. *Failure*: 10 (3d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Prone and Stunned conditions until the end of the angel's next turn. *Success*: Half damage only.
>*NOTE: Consider removing the Stun*

***Spellcasting.*** The angel casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):
* **At Will:** *Disguise Self* (24-hour duration), *Locate Creature*
## Bonus Actions
***Warding Bond (1/Day).*** The angel an choose ne creature it can see within 30 feet to become its ward. The target becomes the angel's ward until it dies or until the angel uses this Bonus Action to choose a different ward. While the angel is within 10 feet of this ward, the ward gains a +4 bonus to saving throws.
## Reactions
***Warding Parry.*** *Trigger:* The angel's ward is targeted by a melee or ranged attack while within 10 feet of the angel. *Response*: The ward adds 4 to its AC, and the angel makes a Spear attack against the triggering attacker if it is within reach.
