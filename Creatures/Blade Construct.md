## Traits
***Bladed Body.*** When a creature starts its turn grappled or being grappled by the construct, it takes 9 (2d8) slashing damage.

***Bloodied - Defiant.*** While bloodied, the construct's attacks deal an additional 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

***Magic Resistance.*** The construct has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Pawn's Bravery.*** During its first turn of combat, the construct's movement speed is doubled.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The construct can make two Slash attacks. It can replace one of these with Metal Claw or Night Slash if available.

***Metal Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage and the construct has advantage on its next attack roll. 

***Night Slash.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. or range 20/80, one target. *Hit*: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. This attack scores a critical on 19-20.

***Slash.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. This attack scores a critical on a 19-20.

***Iron Defense.*** The construct uses the dodge action and gains 11 (2d10) temporary hit points. After the construct scores a critical hit, it can use Iron Defense as a bonus action that turn.
## Bonus Actions
***Pressure.*** *Charisma Saving Throw*: DC 7, Each creature of its choice within 15 ft. *Failure*: The creature can't use its Bonus Action during its next turn.
