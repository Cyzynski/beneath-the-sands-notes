## Traits
***Reactive.*** While the animated armory is not bloodied, it can take one Reaction on every turn of combat.

***Swarm.*** The animated armory can occupy another creature's space and vice versa. The swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny weapon. The swarm can't regenerate hit points or gain temporary hit points.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The animated armory makes two Strikes attacks

***Strikes.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 17 (4d6 + 3) Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing damage (determine randomly). If the swarm is bloodied, this damage is reduced to 10 (2d6 + 3). 
## Reactions
***Riposte.*** *Trigger:* The animated armory is hit by a melee attack roll. *Response:* The animated armory adds 3 to its AC against the attack, possibly turning the hit into a miss. If the attack misses and the swarm is not bloodied, it then makes a Strikes attack against the attacker.
