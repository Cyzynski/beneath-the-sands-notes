## Abilities
**_Sand Form._** The elemental can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing. In addition, the elemental can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. 

***Dust on the Wind.*** The first time the elemental enters a creature's space on a turn **and** whenever another creature ends its turn in the elemental's space, that creature must make a *Constitution Saving Throw* DC 15. *Failure*: The creature is blinded for 1 minute. A creature repeats this saving theow at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

***Glass.*** If the elemental takes more than 15 fire damage at once, it partially glasses; for 1 minute, its speed is reduced by 5 feet and it cannot fly. This effect can stack.
## Actions
**_Multiattack_**. The elemental makes two Slam attacks and can replace one of them with a use of Scour if available.

**_Slam_**. _Melee Attack Roll_: +8, reach 10 ft. _Hit_: 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage.

**_Scour (Recharge 5-6)._** *Constitution Saving Throw*: DC 15, each creature in the elemental's space. 
* *Failure*: 19 (4d6 + 5) Slashing damage and is knocked prone. 
* *Success*: Half damage only.
