## Abilities
**_Air Form._** The elemental can enter a creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch.

***Iron Scent.*** The elemental an pinpoint the location of any ferrous metal within 30 feet of itself.

***Poisonous Air.*** *Constitution Saving Throw*: DC 14, any creature that starts its turn inside the space of the elemental. *Failure*: the target becomes Poisoned until the start of its next turn.

***Unusual Nature.*** The elemental doesn't need air, food, drink, or sleep.
## Actions
**_Multiattack_**. The elemental makes two Corroding Slam attacks.

**_Corroding Slam_**. _Melee Attack Roll_: +7, reach 5ft. 
* _Hit_: 22 (4d8 + 4) Acid damage. 
* *Miss*: *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 14. 
	* *Failure*: A nonmagical metallic item takes a -1 penalty to the AC it offers (armor) or to its attack rolls & damage (weapon). The item is destroyed if the penalty ever reaches -5 or if the AC is reduced to 10. This penalty can be removed by *mending*.

***Oxidizing Whirlwind (1/Rest).*** *Constitution Saving Throw*: DC 14., each creature in a 30-foot cone.
* *Failure*: 22 (4d10) force damage. 
* *Success*: Half damage.
* *Failure or Success*: Nonmagical metallic objects that affected creatures are wearing or carrying take a -1 penalty to the AC it offers (armor) or to its attack rolls & damage (weapon). The item is destroyed if the penalty ever reaches -5 or if the AC is reduced to 10. This penalty can be removed by *mending*.
