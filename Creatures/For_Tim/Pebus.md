## Traits
***Swiss Army Ladle.*** Pebus's multitool is a thing of magic. As as action, he can convert it to make it function as any [artisan's tool](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/equipment:tool) *(This does not grant proficiency with that tool)*. Additionally, he can use it to cast *mending*.

***Quartermaster.*** Pebus has advantage on Intelligence (History) checks made to recall how much of any one thing there is on his ship as well as where it *should* be.

***Pack Tactics.*** Pebus has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of his allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the kobold has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

***Windup Toy.*** Pebus's windup {TOY} functions as a familiar per the *find familiar* spell, minus anything uniquely cool and magical.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** Pebus makes two Multitool or Magnifier w/ Flashlight attacks.

***Multitool.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 9 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.

***Magnifier w/ Flashlight.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +7, range 60 ft. *Hit*: 11 (1d6 + 4) Fire/Radiant/Lightning damage.

***Cooking Oil (1/Day).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*, each creature in a 10-foot square centered on a point within 60 feet. *DC:* 15
* *Failure*: the target has the Prone condition. 
* *Success or Failure*: Flammable grease covers the ground in the area and turns it into Difficult Terrain until someone (probably himself later) cleans it up. A creature enters or ends its turn in the grease must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) or have the Prone condition.
	* If the grease is ignited, it burns for 1 minute, and deals 2d4 fire damage to any creature that fails the Dexterity save, and sets them on fire as well for 1 minute.
## Bonus Actions
***Snacks (2/Rest).*** Pebus provides a delicious snack to an adjacent creature. They regain 2d4 + 4 hit points. He only regains uses during a rest if he cooks replacements.

***Fistful of Cayenne (2/Rest).*** *Constitution Saving Throw*, one creature within 5 ft. *DC:* 15. *Failure*: the target is Blind until the start of Pebus's next turn.
