## Traits
***Substance Abuse.*** Whenever Cap'n ingests alcohol, he gains 1 stack of *B.A.C.* (maximum of 5) for each stack he has, he gains the following "benefits":
* All damage he takes is reduced by 1,
* All Wisdom and Intelligence rolls get -1

While he has no *B.A.C.*, Cap'n gets "the shakes" causing him to have disadvantage on any Dexterity checks and to lose half his movement speed until he gets his fix.

***Tavern Brawler.*** For unarmed strikes, Cap'n can reroll the die if it rolls a 1, but must use the new roll. Once per turn, when he hits a creature with an unarmed strike, he may push that creature 5 ft away.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** Cap'n makes two unarmed strike or improvised weapon attacks in any combination.

***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 7 (1d4 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. 
* Instead of dealing damage, he may force the target to make a DC 15 Acrobatics or Athletics check (its choice) or be [grappled](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#Grappling).

***Improvised Weapon.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll*: +7, range 20/60 ft., *Hit*: 7 (1d4 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. 

***Party Trick (Requires 1+ B.A.C.).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* Each creature in a 15 ft. cone, DC 13.
* *Failure*: Cap'n breathes out a blast of flame, dealing 3d6 fire damage.
* *Success*: half damage only
* *Success or Failure*: Cap'n loses 1 *B.A.C.*

***Purge (Requires 3+ B.A.C.).*** *Constitution Saving Throw*: One adjacent creature, DC 13. 
* *Failure*: Cap'n expels caustic bile on the target, dealing 3`X`d8 Acid damage where `X` is his current *B.A.C.*
* *Success*: half damage only
* *Success or Failure*: Cap'n loses all remaining *B.A.C.* and gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#ExhaustionCondition).
## Bonus Actions
***Imbibe (5/Day).*** Cap'n takes a heavy draw from his flask, gaining 1 *B.A.C.*
