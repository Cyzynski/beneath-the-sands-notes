## Traits
***Mercurial.*** The animated weapon has three different forms - Greatsword, Greataxe, and Maul. Changing forms only affects its attacks and can be done each round as a bonus action.

## Actions
***Greatsword - Slash.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 9 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage. *Miss*: 3 Slashing damage.

***Greataxe - Slash.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 9 (1d12 + 3) Slashing damage. Then, the greataxe immediately makes another attack against another creature within 5ft of the first. On a hit, that attack does 6 (1d12) Slashing damage.

***Maul - Smash.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 9 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage and make a DC 13 Constitution Saving Throw or be knocked Prone.

## Bonus Actions
***Convert.*** The animated weapon transforms into a different one of it's modes.
