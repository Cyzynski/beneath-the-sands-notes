**Immunities** poison, psychic, Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60ft, passive Perception 9
**Languages** understands Draconic and Zmokan, but can't speak.
**CR** 2 (PB +2) #CR/2
## Traits
***Bloodied - Overheating.*** While bloodied, it gains resistance to fire damage, but whenever it takes 5 or more fire damage, it gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.aidedd.org/en/rules/conditions/). All of the construct's damaging effects deal an additional 1d6 fire damage while it is Overhearing.

***Chest Cavity.*** The scavenger an store up to three Small or smaller objects in its body. While incapacitated, other creatures can open the storage to retrieve objects. Mundane objects inside the cavity are destroyed when the scavenger explodes.

***Volatile.*** The scavenger explodes when it dies. *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 11, each creature in a 5-foot emanation originating from the scavenger. *Failure*: 10 (3d6) force damage. *Success*: Half damage.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The scavenger makes two Claw attacks.

***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 9 (2d6 + 2) Slashing damage and the target is grappled (escape DC 12). This attack deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.

***Break and Assimilate (Recharge 4-6).*** The scavenger destroys a non-magical Small or smaller object made out of metal it is carrying or takes out from its Chest Cavity and regains 9 (2d8) hit points.
## Bonus Actions
***Spin and Yank.*** *Strength Saving Throw*: DC 12, one creature grappled by the scavenger. *Failure*: the target is pushed 10 feet in a random direction, knocked prone, and the scavenger rips an object the target was carrying from its posession.
