## Traits
***Bloodied - Overheating.*** While bloodied, it gains resistance to fire damage, but whenever it takes 5 or more fire damage, it gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.aidedd.org/en/rules/conditions/). All of the construct's damaging effects deal an additional 1d6 fire damage while it is Overhearing.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The drone can use its Self-Replicate. It then makes one claw attack.

***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage. If the target isn't a construct, plant, undead, or ooze, the drone regains an equal amount of hit points. This attack deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.

***Self-Replicate.*** The ripper drone takes 5 necrotic damage, and creates a second ripper drone, appearing within 5 feet of the drone which spawned it. It only has 10 hit points, and has a maximum of 15.
