## Traits
**Magic Resistance.** The chull has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

---
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The chull makes two attacks, using any combination of the following:

***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +9, reach 15 ft. *Hit*: 22 (3d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 15). The chull can only grapple a creature if it has a free claw.

***Boulder.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +9, range 60/240 ft. *Hit*: 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it is knocked Prone.

***Fling.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one creature it is Grappling and any other creature within 60 feet as the chull throws the creature in its claw. *Failure*: 18 (4d8) Bludgeoning damage and knocked Prone. *Success*: The thrown creature takes half damage only, the target creature takes no damage.

******
## Bonus Actions
***Lure.*** *Charisma Saving Throw*: DC 15, one creature within 120 ft. *Failure*: During it's next turn, the target must use all of their movement to move straight toward the chull and make at least one attack against it. Creatures immune or resistant to the charmed condition are immune or resistant to this effect, respectively.

---
## Legendary Actions (2)
***Stride.*** The chull moves up to half of its movement without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Whirling Slam (Costs 2 Actions).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 15, each creature in a 15 foot emanation. *Failure*: 13 (2d6 + 6) Bludgeoning Damage and knocked Prone. It's AC permanently decreases by 1 as it loses pieces of stone armor.
