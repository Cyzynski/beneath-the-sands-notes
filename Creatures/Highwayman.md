## Abilities
**_Nick._** If the Highwayman hits a creature with a Dagger or Revolver attack, it may make an attack with its offhand weapon against the same target as a Bonus Action.
## Actions
**_Dagger_**. _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll_: +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. _Hit_: 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.

**_Revolver_**. _Ranged Attack Roll_: +4, range 100/400 ft. _Hit_: 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage. The revolver holds 6 rounds, after all are fired, the Highwayman must spend an action to reload.
