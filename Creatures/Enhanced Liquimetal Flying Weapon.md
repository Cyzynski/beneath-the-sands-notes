## Traits
***Mercurial.*** The animated weapon has three different forms - Greatsword, Greataxe, and Maul. Changing forms only affects its attacks and can be done each round as a bonus action.
## Actions
***Greatsword - Slash.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage. *Miss*: 4 Slashing damage.

***Greataxe - Slash.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 11 (1d12 + 4) Slashing damage. Then, the greataxe immediately makes another attack against another creature within 5ft of the first. On a hit, that attack does 7 (1d12) Slashing damage.

***Maul - Smash.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage and make a DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw or be knocked Prone.

## Bonus Actions
***Split.*** If the weapon has at least 10 hit points, it splits into two. The new weapon appears in an unoccupied space within 5ft. Divide its current hit points evenly between the two weapons. The new one may immediately make an attack action.

***Convert.*** The animated weapon transforms into a different one of it's modes.
