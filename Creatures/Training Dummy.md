## Traits
***Adaptive Learning.*** Whenever the dummy takes damage, it gains a stacking 2 damage reduction to that type (maximum of 10). These resistances reset at the end of combat.

***Perfect Balance.*** The dummy can't be knocked Prone or moved unwillingly unless it chooses to be.

***Efficient Motion.*** The dummy ignores Difficult Terrain and can move through the space of creature up to one size larger than it.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dummy makes two Slam attacks.

***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. 

***Ringing Blow (Recharge 5-6).*** The dummy makes one Slam attack. On a hit, the target must make a *Constitution Saving Throw* DC 15. *Failure*: The target is Stunned until the dummy's next turn.
## Bonus Actions
***Shift Stance.*** The dummy adopts one of the following stances until the start of its next turn:
* **Defensive:** The dummy gains +2 to its AC
* **Aggressive:** The dummy's attacks do an additional 2 damage.
* **Mobile:** The dummy's speed increases by 10 feet.
## Reactions
***Predictive Dodge.*** When a creature missed the dummy with an attack roll, the dummy moves up to half of its movement without provoking opportunity attacks.
## Legendary Actions (3)
***Move.*** The dummy moves up to half of it's Speed.

***Analyze.*** The dummy immediately uses Shift Stance.

***Slam.*** The dummy makes one Slam attack.
