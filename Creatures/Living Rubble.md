## Traits
***False Appearance.*** If the rubble is motionless at the start of combat, it has advantage on its initiative roll. Moreover, if a creature hasn't observed the rubble move or act, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check to discern that it is more than just a pile of debris.

***Unusual Nature.*** The rubble does not need food, drink, air, or sleep.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The rubble makes one Debris Slam attack and uses its Wall of Rubble once.

***Debris Slam.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll*: +7, reach 10 ft. or range 30/60 ft. *Hit*: 17 (2d12 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. 

***Wall of Rubble.*** The rubble casts *wall of stone*. The spell can only create ten 10x10-foot wide panels within 120ft, doesn't require concentration, and lasts until the start of its next turn.
## Bonus Actions
***Wall Crumble.*** The rubble chooses a wall segment of its Wall of Rubble that is within 5ft of a creature. *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 15, the creature next to the segment. *Failure*: 13 (3d8) bludgeoning damage. *Failure or Success*: The chosen wall segment is destroyed.
## Reactions
***Destructive Collapse.*** *Trigger:* A creature within 5ft of a segment of the Wall of Rubble makes an attack roll. *Response*: The rubble uses Wall Crumble against that creature. The creature can choose to make its triggering attack roll with disadvantage. If it does, it has advantage on the saving throw against Wall Crumble.
