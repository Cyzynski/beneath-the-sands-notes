---
**AC** 17
**HP** 200
**Initiative** +0 (10)
**Speed** 30ft., climb 30ft.

**Immunities** poison, psychic, Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60ft, passive Perception 10
**Languages** understands Draconic and Zmokan, but can't speak.
**CR** 11 (PB +3) #CR/11
## Traits
***Bloodied - Overheating.*** When the dragon becomes bloodied for the first time, it recharges its Rust Breath. While bloodied, it gains resistance to fire damage, but whenever it takes 5 or more fire damage, it gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.aidedd.org/en/rules/conditions/). All of the dragon's damaging effects deal an additional 2d6 fire damage while it is Overhearing.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three Rend or Steel Scale attacks. It can replace one with a use of Scrap Barrier if it is available. 

***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 18 (3d8 + 5) Slashing damage. This attack deals an additional 7 (2d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.

***Rust Breath (1/Day).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 16, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure*: 31 (7d8) acid damage and each metal object a target is wearing takes a -1 penalty to the AC it offers or its attack rolls. Armor is destroyed if its AC is reduced to 10 and weapons are destroyed if the penalty ever reaches -5. The penalty can be removed by casting the spell *mending* or an hour of repair with the appropriate tools per level of penalty. *Success*: Half damage only.

***Scrap Barrier (Recharge 4-6).*** The dragon gains 2d8 temporary hit points, whenever a creature hits the dragon with a melee attack while it has any of those temporary hit points that duration, it takes 10 (4d4) slashing damage. This effect deals an additional 7 (2d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.
## Bonus Actions
***Overclock.*** The dragon can make one additional Rend attack as part of its Multiattack this turn. Then next turn, it makes one fewer Rend attack and it can't use this ability.
## Legendary Actions (Overheating Only) (2)
**_Rend._** The dragon makes one Rend attack against a creature in range.

**_Vent Steam._** _Constitution Saving Throw_: DC 13, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation. _Failure_: 14 (4d6) fire damage. _Success_: Half damage only.
