## Actions
**_Multiattack_**. The Highwayman makes two Dagger or Volley Gun attacks in any combination.

**_Dagger_**. _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll_: +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. _Hit_: 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.

**_Volley Gun_**.  _Ranged Attack Roll_: +5, range 60/100 ft. _Hit_: 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage. The volley gun holds 100 rounds, after all are fired, the Highwayman must spend an action to reload.

**_Salvo_**. _Dexterity Saving Throw_: DC 15, all creatures in a 10-foot Cube within 60ft. _Failure_: the target takes 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage.
Regardless of outcome, the Volley Gun uses 10 ammunition.
