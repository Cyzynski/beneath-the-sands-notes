---

**Initiative** +2 (12)
**Speed** 0ft. (can only pivot in place)

**Immunities** poison, psychic, Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60ft, passive Perception 10
**Languages** understands Draconic and Zmokan, but can't speak.
**CR** 5 (PB +3) #CR/5

## Traits
***Bloodied - Overheating.*** When the dragon becomes bloodied for the first time, it recharges its Rust Breath. While bloodied, it gains resistance to fire damage, but whenever it takes 5 or more fire damage, it gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.aidedd.org/en/rules/conditions/). All of the dragon's damaging effects deal an additional 1d6 fire damage while it is Overhearing.
## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes two Rend or Steel Scale attacks. It can replace one with a use of Scrap Barrier if it is available. 

***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage. This attack deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.

***Steel Scale.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +5, range 20/60 ft. *Hit*: 10 (2d8 + 2) Piercing damage. This attack deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied. *Hit or Miss*: The dragon takes 1d6 Force damage.

***Rust Breath (1/Day).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure*: 31 (7d8) acid damage and each metal object a target is wearing takes a -1 penalty to the AC it offers or its attack rolls. Armor is destroyed if its AC is reduced to 10 and weapons are destroyed if the penalty ever reaches -5. The penalty can be removed by casting the spell *mending* or an hour of repair with the appropriate tools per level of penalty. *Success*: Half damage only.

***Scrap Barrier (Recharge 5-6).*** The dragon gains 2d8 temporary hit points, whenever a creature hits the dragon with a melee attack while it has any of those temporary hit points that duration, it takes 5 (2d4) slashing damage. This effect deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.
## Bonus Actions
***Overclock.*** The dragon can make one additional Rend attack as part of its Multiattack this turn. Then next turn, it makes one fewer Rend or Steel Scale attacks and it can't use this ability.
## Legendary Actions (Overheating Only) (2)
**_Fabricate (Cost: 2 Actions)._** 2 [[Clockwork Drone]]s spawn on opposite sides of the room, rolling out of chutes in the ceiling.

**_Rend._** The dragon makes one Rend attack against a creature in range.

**_Steel Scale._** The dragon makes one Steel Scale attack against a creature in range.

**_Vent Steam._** _Constitution Saving Throw_: DC 13, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation. _Failure_: 7 (2d6) fire damage. _Success_: Half damage only.
