---
**AC** 16

**Initiative** +2 (12)
**Speed** 0ft. (can only pivot in place)

**Immunities** poison, psychic, Charmed, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60ft, passive Perception 10
**Languages** understands Draconic and Zmokan, but can't speak.
**CR** 4 (PB +2) #CR/3

## Traits
***Bloodied - Overheating.*** When the dragon becomes bloodied for the first time, it recharges its Rust Breath. While bloodied, it gains resistance to fire damage, but whenever it takes 5 or more fire damage, it gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.aidedd.org/en/rules/conditions/). All of the dragon's damaging effects deal an additional 1d6 fire damage while it is Overhearing.

## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes two Rend or Steel Scale attacks. It can replace one with a use of Scrap Barrier if it is available. 

***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 9 (1d10 + 4) Slashing damage. This attack deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.

***Steel Scale.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +5, range 20/60 ft. *Hit*: 10 (2d8 + 2) Piercing damage. This attack deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied. *Hit or Miss*: The dragon takes 1d6 Force damage.

***Rust Breath (1/Day).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw*: DC 13, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure*: 22 (4d10) acid damage and each object a target is wearing takes a -1 penalty to the AC it offers or its attack rolls. Armor is destroyed if its AC is reduced to 10 and weapons are destroyed if the penalty ever reaches -5. The penalty can be removed by casting the spell *mending* or an hour of repair with the appropriate tools per level of penalty. *Success*: Half damage only.

***Scrap Barrier (Recharge 5-6).*** Ranged attacks have disadvantage against the dragon until the end of its next turn. Additionally, whenever a creature hits the dragon with a melee attack during that duration, it takes 5 (4d4) slashing damage. This effect deals an additional 3 (1d6) Fire damage while the dragon is bloodied.

## Bonus Actions
***Overclock.*** The dragon can make one additional Rend attack as part of Multiattack this turn. Then next turn, it makes one fewer Rend attack and it can't use this ability.
