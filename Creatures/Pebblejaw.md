## Traits
***Hold Breath.*** The Pebblejaw can hold its breath for 10 minutes.

***Pack Tactics.*** The pebblejaw has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the pebblejaw's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.

## Actions
***Slowing Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage, and the target is poisoned until the the end of its next turn. While poisoned this way, the target's speed is reduced by 10 feet and it can't use reactions.

***Pebble Spit.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 11, one creature within 20 feet. *Failure:* 3 (1d4 +1) bludgeoning damage.

***Alarm Shriek.*** The pebblejaw emits  shriek audible within 300 feet of itself. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature (except for other pebblejaws) in a 10-foot emanation originating from the pebblejaw. *Failure:* The target becomes deafened until the end of its next turn.
