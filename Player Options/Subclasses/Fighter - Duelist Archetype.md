_(From Gunslinger’s Guide to Rexfald)_

_Duel with Deadly Swiftness_

Duelists host reflexes at the pinnacle of human ability, excelling at catching their foes off-guard and finishing fights before they begin. Motivated by the thrill of competition, duelists seek glory and renown in the pursuit of battlefield perfection.

### Level 3: Snap Reflexes

Your reflexes are the envy of your peers. When you roll Initiative, you can expend a use of your Second Wind to add 1d10 to your initiative roll. The first time you hit with a weapon attack on a turn, the target takes extra damage equal to 1/3 your initiative (rounded up). When you attack a creature that has not yet taken a turn in initiative, you deal this extra damage with every hit, rather than just the first one.

### Level 3: Coercive Arms

You become adept at incorporating weapon flourishes and tricks into your speech. Whenever you take the Influence action, you can add your Initiative bonus to the check, provided that you incorporate a weapon into the action.

### Level 7: Deflecting Blow

When you or a creature within range or reach of a weapon you are holding is hit by an attack, you can use your Reaction to attempt to deflect it. Make a weapon attack roll. You have Advantage on this attack roll if your initiative is higher than the hostile attacker’s initiative. If the total is equal to or higher than the triggering attack’s total, you deflect it and negate all of that attack's damage and effects. If your attack roll is lower, the attack still hits, but the damage dealt by the attack is reduced by an amount equal to your Initiative.

You may use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining all expended uses on a short or long rest.

### Level 10: Unshakable Focus

You cannot be Surprised. You have Advantage on Initiative rolls and ignore the Charmed, Frightened, and Stunned conditions if your Initiative is higher than the DC for the effect that imposed the condition.

### Level 10: Duelist’s Leer

Your leering gaze strikes fear into the hearts of your opponents. One creature that you can see before the start of combat must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC equal to 10 + your Initiative Bonus). On a failure, the creature is Surprised at the start of combat and Frightened of you until the end of its first turn. Creatures Immune to being Frightened or Surprised, or that cannot see you, automatically succeed on the saving throw.

This ability can be taken again as a Bonus Action in combat, forcing the target to make the Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature is Frightened of you until the end of your next turn. A creature with a lower Initiative than you has Disadvantage on the saving throw.

### Level 15: Precise Disarm

When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can attempt to disarm it. The target makes a Constitution saving throw (DC equal to 8 plus the attack modifier of a weapon you are holding in hand). On a failure, one weapon or object the target is holding flies from their grasp, landing at a space of your choice within 30 feet of the target. If the target cannot be disarmed they automatically succeed on the saving throw. If you are standing within 30 feet of the disarmed target, you can have the dropped item land in your grasp, provided that you have a free hand to catch it. You can use this feature only once per turn.

### Level 18: Showdown

Heightened to the pinnacle of ability, you attack with a flurry of blinding speed. As a Magic Action, you can expend a use of your Action Surge to cast a special version of the spell _Time Stop_. When you do so, the spell requires a somatic component instead of a verbal component and its effect does not end when you affect a creature with a weapon attack.
