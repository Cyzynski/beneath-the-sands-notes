_(From Gunslinger’s Guide to Rexfald)_

_Endure endlessly in the face of adversity_

This oath is taken by those who expect lives of hardship and death. This oath is also claimed by those who will not allow themselves to fall until their will is done. Many paladins that take up this oath do so in service of those who survive by their hand, while others claim this oath to see their personal will enacted before their demise.

**Tenets**

* Embrace hardship as a part of life
* Teach others how to thrive on their own
* Never give in to death

### Level 3: Stoic Aegis

As a Magic Action, you can expend your Channel Divinity to shroud a creature you touch with a holy blessing of fortitude. For the next 8 hours, the warded creature gains the following benefits:

* It has Advantage on saving throws against Exhaustion and on maintaining its Concentration.
* It gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Charisma modifier at the start of each of its turns.
* I can use its Reaction when it takes damage to dismiss the Aegis and negate all damage taken.

### Level 7: Aura of Endurance

Whenever you or an ally takes damage while in your Aura of Protection, the damage is reduced by an amount equal to your Charisma modifier.

### Level 15: Zealous Perseverance

When you or a creature under the effect of your Stoic Aegis take damage but are not reduced to 0 hit points or killed outright, you can use a Reaction to expend a Paladin spell slot to reduce the damage taken by an amount equal to 5 times the level of the spell slot expended.

Once per Short Rest, when you or a creature within your Aura of Protection makes a Death Saving Throw, you can turn the roll into a 20 on the d20.

### Level 20: Immortal Paragon

You fortify yourself with the immortal vigor of the gods. As a Bonus Action, you gain the benefits below for 1 minute. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (no action required).

**Divine Protector.** Whenever an ally within your Aura of Protection takes damage less than your current Hit Points, you can choose to take all of the damage instead (no action required).

**Immortal.** You cannot die.

**Immutable.** You are immune to any spells or effects that would alter your form against your will.
