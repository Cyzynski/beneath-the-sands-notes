Warriors of the Sands are truly at home in the Deep Desert, channeling their reserves to harness the power of the Sands. They are able to exert their will over the Sands to create shapes and even weapons from it.

### Level 3: Sand Control

Through rigorous training you have learned to draw on the power of the Sands, gaining the following benefits:

**Sand Whips:** As a bonus action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to create a magic Whip out of Sand. A whip created in this considered a Monk Weapon for you and deals Force damage. The whip lasts for 10 minutes or until you drop it.

When you hit a creature with this whip, you may have the target make a Strength Saving Throw. On a failed save, they are pulled up to 10 ft towards you.

**Sand Ropes:** As a bonus action, you can create up to 50 feet of rope out of sand. If you’re ever not touching the rope, it disintegrates.

### Level 3: Limited Tremorsense

As long as you are in sand, you gain Tremorsense to a distance of 10 ft.

### Level 6: Sandblast

As a Magic action, you can spend 2 focus points to create a shockwave. Each creature in a 20-foot Cube originating from you makes a Constitution Saving Throw. On a failed save, a creature takes Force damage equal to 3 rolls of your martial arts die and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

Before or after you take this action, you can make one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action.

### Level 11: Sandform

As a Magic action, you can wreath your body sand for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated. While you are altered in this way, you gain the following benefits:

**Protective Barrier:** You gain temporary hit points equal to a number of rolls of your martial arts die equal to your Widom modifier. These temporary hit points last until your Sandform ends.

**Burrowing:** You gain a burrowing speed equal to half your walking speed as long as you are in sand.

You can choose to end this early as an action. When you do, you may use your Sandblast feature without expending any Focus Points, dealing additional damage equal to the number of temporary hit points you have remaining from this feature.

Before or after you take this action, you can make one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action.

You can use this ability once, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest, or by spending 2 Focus Points (no action required).
