_Distill the Power of the Moon_

The Circle of Moonshine worships and encourages the process of fermentation, working under the cover of night to brew alcohol with the magical aid of moonglow. These Druids distill pure moonlight into alcohol by means of special druidic jugs. By distilling these spirits, these Druids seek to create medicine and spread merriment wherever they go.

### Level 3: Moonshiner
You have been trained in the alchemical ways of a moonshiner, a druid who turns light into potent alcohols. You gain the following features.
* ***Brewer's Tools Proficiency***. You gain proficiency with brewer's tools, or another tool of your choice if you already have it.
* ***Shine Resistance***. You are Resistant to Radiant and Poison damage and have Advantage on saving throws against being Blinded or Poisoned.
* ***Infused Spells***. Your Druid spells become infused with the power of your moonshine. Whenever you deal damage with a Druid spell, you can change all of its damage to Radiant or Poison damage. A creature that takes Radiant or Poison damage from one of your leveled spells must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be Poisoned for 1 minute. A creature Poisoned this way can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on a success.
* ***Lunar Jug***. You carry a special jug covered in Druidic Runes that distills magical moonshine. When you cast a Druid spell, the jug fills with a number of d4s equal to the level of the spell cast. With the jug in hand, you can use a Bonus Action and choose a number of d4s in the jug to drink. Roll the chosen d4s and heal a number of Hit Points equal to the result. Whenever you heal a creature with a Druid spell while your jug is in hand, you can expend a number of these d4s to increase the amount healed, rolling and adding them to the total. The maximum number of d4s the jug can hold is equal to 2 times your Druid level. When you finish a Long Rest while the jug is holding fewer d4s than your Druid level, the jug gains d4s until it is holding a number equal to your Druid level.

### Level 6: Blinding Shine
Whenever you Poison a creature, it also gains the Blinded condition. If you would give the Poisoned condition to a creature immune to being Poisoned, it still makes the save against being Poisoned, becoming Blinded on a failure instead.

### Level 10: Distilled Incantations
You can distil extra power into your spells when you case them. When you cast a leveled spell with your Lunar Jug in hand, you can expend a Wild Shape use to increase the spell cast by a number of levels equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). You cannot increase the level beyond the your highest-level Spell Slot. Any save DCs for the Poisoned or Blinded condition imposed by this spell also increase by your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1).

### Level 14: Soulshine
The light-infused magic in your moonshine is able to fortify and fracture the very souls of creatures. You gain access to the following features:
* ***Fortified Brew***. When you heal a creature with a Druid spell or your Lunar Jug, you can make them Immune to one damage type or condition for the next minute. A creature target this way cannot be targeted again with this feature until it finishes a Long Rest.
* ***Hangover***. When you give the Poisoned or Blinded condition to a creature, you can make it Vulnerable to your choice of Poison or Radiant damage for as long as it remains Poisoned or Blinded.

You may use each of these features once, regaining any expended uses when you finish a Long Rest, or by expending a 7th level or higher spell slot to regain use of one of these features (no action required).
