_(From Gunslinger’s Guide to Rexfald)_

_Duel mages with arcane alacrity_

Great wizards hail from many different paths, but among all mages, the one constant trait is a vivacious competitive spirit. These rivalries breed a special wizarding discipline; one whose focus is not on one specific school or study, but instead mastering magic to defeat arcane competitors and claim victory above all else.

### Level 3: Mage’s Prescience

As a magic action, you can open your mind to the magic leylines surrounding a creature within 60ft of you. You immediately learn the following information about the target:

* Whether or not the target is able to cast spells or use magical abilities.
* Any signature spells or unique abilities the target possesses.
* The source of the target’s magic, such as their bloodline, a powerful entity, a magic item, or another source.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Level 3: Spellarm

You crystallize a personalized mote of arcane power in a rod, staff, or wand – inscribing it with arcane runes that transform it into a Spellarm. Each crafted Spellarm is unique, counting as an arcane focus for your spells. You can have a total number of Spellarms equal to your Intelligence modifier.

Certain abilities have a chance to break your Spellarm. A broken Spellarm cannot be used. You can repair a broken Spellarm, transform an existing arcane focus into a Spellarm, or create a new one with one hour of work, which can be during a Short Rest. When you do, select a Wizard cantrip you know to imbue into the Spellarm. Whenever you have that Spellarm in hand, you can case that cantrip even if you do not currently know it.

Certain abilities cause a Spellarm to become volatile Whenever you use a volatile Spellarm to cast a spell or use Disarming Jolt, you must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw (DC equal to 10 or twice the level of the expended spell slot – whichever is higher) to not break the Spellarm.

While you are in possession of a functioning Spellarm, you gain the following benefits:

**Arcane Alacrity.** When you roll Initiative, you can expend a spell slot to gain a bonus to your roll equal to twice the level of the spell slot expended. If you are Surprised when you expend this spell slot, you are no longer Surprised. If you are not holding a Spellarm when you expend this spell slot, you can immediately teleport a functional one into your hand. Expending your highest-level spell slot for this feature causes the Spellarm to become volatile until you finish a Rest.

**Disarming Jolt.** With a functional Spellarm in hand, you can use a Bonus Action to make a ranged spell attack against a creature within 60ft. On a hit, you deal Force damage equal to 1d6 + your Wizard level and the target must make a Constitution saving throw against your Wizard spell save DC. On a failure, the target drops one item that it is holding, losing attunement to any item it drops. A creature that has not yet taken a turn in Initiative has Disadvantage on the saving throw. Using this Bonus Action causes the Spellarm to become volatile until the end of combat.

**Quick Cantrips.** When you first draw your Spellarm, or at the start of your first turn in combat if your Spellarm is in hand, you can immediately case the cantrip stored within it (no action required). Once you case this cantrip, the Spellarm becomes volatile until the end of combat.

### Level 6: Swiftcast

Any creature that would take no damage on a successful save against one of your spells instead takes Force damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

### Level 10: Hex-off

Whenever a creature that you can see targets you with a spell or magical effect while you are holding a Spellarm, you can use your Reaction to spend a spell slot and directly abjure it. The creature that cast the spell makes a saving throw against with its spellcasting ability against your Wizard spell save DC. On a failure, the spell or magical effect is dispelled and has no effect, and you can immediately use Disarming Jolt against the spellcaster. On a success, you are hit by the spell or magical effect as normal, the Spellarm you used for this Reaction breaks, and you regain the spell slot expended to use this ability.

### Level 14: Arcane Barrage

You unleash every mote of arcane power stored within your Spellarm as a Magic action, casting a barrage of spells in quick succession. Choose 1 Wizard spell you have prepared with a casting time of 1 Action that doesn’t require concentration. You can immediately case this spell a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus as part of this action, paying the spell’s material costs and expending spell slots for each cast.

If you cannot provide a valid spell slot or material components for a casting this action immediately ends.

After you finish casting the last spell, you must make an Intelligence saving throw against a DC equal to the total of all spell slot level expended this way. On a failure, the Spellarm you are holding breaks.

This feature can only be used once per rest.
