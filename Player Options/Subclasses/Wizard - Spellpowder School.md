_Brandish firearms and unleash arcane power_

### Level 3: Arcane Armament

You learn to construct a weapon to focus your damaging spells. When you gain this feature, you choose of the three available options: **Blunderbuss**, **Flintlock**, or **Hand Cannon**. While this weapon is on your person you are considered proficient with it, and you may use your intelligence modifier for the purposes of attack and damage rolls. Additionally, you Arcane Armament counts as an arcane focus for the purposes of casing wizard spells, and ignores the ammunition property.

You may perform a 1-hour ritual requiring 50gp of materials to replace a lost Arcane Armament or to change its for into one of the other options. When you complete this ritual, your previous Arcane Armament is destroyed.

#### Blunderbuss

**Damage:** 2d4 piercing                    **Range:** 15/40

**Properties:** Ammunition, Two-Handed

**Boomstick.** Attacks made with this weapon ignore Disadvantage if made within 5ft of an enemy. When you cast a spell using this weapon as a spellcasting focus that has an area of effect and a range of Self, any creature within 5ft of you makes the saving throw with Disadvantage.

#### Flintlock

**Ramage:** 1d8 piercing                    **Range:** 100/400

**Properties:** Ammunition, One-Handed

**Spellshot.** When you cast a spell using this weapon as a spellcasting focus that requires one or more attack rolls, or the Magic Missile spell, you may add your proficiency bonus to one of the damage rolls.

#### Hand Cannon

**Damage:** 1d6 force                         **Range:** 80/160

**Properties:** Ammunition, Two-Handed

**Magic Mortar.** When you hit a creature with an attack roll made with this weapon, each creature within 5ft. of it also takes 1d6 force damage. Additionally, when you cast a spell using this weapon as a spellcasting focus that affects an area with a range other than self, you can choose to center the area on one creature. That creature has disadvantage on the first save made to resist the spell.

### Level 6: Quickdraw

As a Bonus Action, you may shift your Arcane Armament into any of the other forms, no components required. Additionally, you can attack twice whenever you take the Atack action on your turn.

### Level 10: Countershot

As a reaction, when a creature within the short range of your Arcane Armament makes a ranged weapon or spell attack, you may make an attack against the spell or missile. The AC of the missile or spell is equal to the initial attack roll. On a hit, the spell or missile is shot out of the air, dispelled, and causing no harm to its intended target.

### Level 14: Arcane Firepower

Each Arcane Armament gains access to an Arcane Firepower ability, these abilities can be used a number of times per day equal to your Proficiency Bonys across all of your armaments.

#### Blunderbuss

**Full Choke.** The Boomstick property extends to the short range of the blunderbuss, and any creatures that fail the save take an additional 1d6 damage per level of the spell.

#### Flintlock

**Fan the Hammer.** Once per turn, when you use your action to cast a spell that requires an attack roll you may immediately cast another spell of 1st level or lowe that requires an attack roll.

#### Hand Cannon

**Dangerous Trajectory.** When you center the area of effect of a spell on a creature, you may instead choose to make an attack roll against them. On a hit, treat it as if the creature failed the saving throw, and on a miss as if they passed. If it is a critical hit, the creature takes additional damage equal to your wizard level. All other creatures in range make their saves as normal.
