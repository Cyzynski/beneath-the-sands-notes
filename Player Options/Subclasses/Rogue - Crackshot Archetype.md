_(From Gunslinger’s Guide to Rexfald)_

_Astound onlookers with unerring aim_

A crackshot is a master marksman, whose unparalleled precision has been honed through years of relentless practice. These rogues pursue occupations as bounty hunters, duelists, entertainers, and other professions where a sharp eye and lethal aim are crucial for success.

### Level 3: Keen Eye

Your weapons do not suffer Disadvantage from being at Long Range. You can see objects within 120ft with the same clarity as if you saw them up close, and you can see clearly up to 1 mile.

### Level 3: Ace Marksman

You gain a pool of d4s equal to your Dexterity Modifier (minimum of 1). After you make an attack that you have Sneak Attack with, you can spend any number of these d4s to increase your attack roll. Whenever you hit with an attack you have Sneak Attack on, you deal extra damage to the target for each number higher than the Armor Class of the target when you hit it, to a maximum equal to twice your Rogue level.

Whenever you make a Perception check, you can also expend any number of these d4s to increase the check.

You regain all expended d4s whenever you finish a Short or Long Rest. The dice increase in value when you hit Rogue levels 7 (d6), 12 (d8), 16 (d10), and 20 (d12).

### Level 9: Trick Shot:

Whenever you attack an object, you automatically hit. When you make an attack against an object or creature you have Sneak Attack on, you ignore all cover between you and the target.

### Level 13: Bullseye

You gain the benefit of Sneak Attack whenever you score a Critical Hit. If your attack roll total is greater than 30, you score a Critical Hit even if you did not roll a 20 on the die.

You regain one expended Ace Marksman die whenever you score a Critical Hit.

### Level 17: Killzone

You can target objects and creatures you can see with your ranged weapon attacks regardless of distance.

Once per turn, when a creature you can sense moves within the range of your weapon without taking the Disengage action, you can expend an Ace Marksman die to make a ranged weapon attack against it with Advantage.
