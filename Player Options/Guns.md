# Weapon Masteries
## Spread
If you hit a creature with a ranged attack roll using this weapon, you can make a ranged attack roll with the weapon against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is also within your reach. On a hit, the second creature takes the weapon’s damage, but don’t add your ability modifier to that damage unless that modifier is negative. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.
# Weapon Properties
## Concealed
While this weapon is equipped, it cannot be detected by the Search action.
## Loading
Because of the time required to load this weapon, you can fire only one piece of ammunition from it when you use an action, bonus action, or reaction to fire it, regardless of the number of attacks you can normally make.
## Preparation
You must spend a Bonus Action before using the Attack action with this weapon on a turn.
## Reload [X]
You can fire this weapon X times before it must be reloaded. You can spend an Action or Bonus Action to do so, provided you have a free hand.
## Salvo
As an action, you can expend 10 pieces of a Salvo weapon’s ammunition to spray shots in a 10-foot Cube within the weapon’s normal range. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take damage.
