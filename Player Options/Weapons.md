| Bayonet | 1d6 s. | Special | Vex | 3 lbs. | 4 GP |

* The Bayonet can be attached to any firearm with the two-handed or versatile property. While that weapon is wielded in two hands, it can function as a simple melee weapon that deals 1d6 piercing damage and has the reach property.
