*Level 2 Conjuration (Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From Many Worlds Tavern)

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 120ft (20 ft. Cylinder)
**Components.** V, S, M (A thrown weapon or piece of ammunition, which the spell consumes)
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You throw a nonmagical weapon or shoot a piece of nonmagical ammunition into the air, which is duplicated to create a barrage in a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-tall cylinder. The weapons or ammunition continue to rain down on the area of effect for the duration of the spell before disappearing at the end of each round.

Any creature that starts their turn or moves through the area of effect must make a *Dexterity Saving Throw*, taking 3d6 damage of the type dealt by the weapon used on a failed save, or half that on a success. If a creature moves less than half their speed during their turn, they gain advantage on the saving throw. A creature wielding a shield or benefitting from cover takes no damage on a successful save.

Each turn that you continue to concentrate on the spell, you must consume another piece of ammunition as a bonus action or the spell ends.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2nd.
