*Level 2 Transmutation (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard, Warlock)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Touch
**Components.** V, S, M (rust from a well-loved item)
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You touch a small or tiny non-magical object and sunder it. A rope snaps, a sack falls to tatters, a wooden board splinters, and a steel weapon rusts. Any object targeted by this spell becomes unusable for its intended purpose.

If the object targeted is being worn or carried by a creature, you must make a melee spell attack roll against that creature's AC. On a hit, the spell functions as if they were not holding or wearing the object.

Objects broken or rendered useless by this spell can be repaired with the mending spell or similar magic.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a 3rd or 4th-level slot, the object can be medium or smaller. When you case this spell using a 5th or 6th-level slot, the object an be large or smaller. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th or 8th-level, the object can be huge or smaller. When you case this spell using a 9th-level slot, the object can be any size.
