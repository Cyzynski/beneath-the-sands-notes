*Level 2 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 30 ft
**Components.** V, S, M (a handful of sand)
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You create an illusory duplicate of yourself formed from animated sand. The clone appears in an unoccupied space within range and has AC 12 and 15 hit points. 

On your turn, you can mentally command the clone to move up to 30 feet and take simple actions using your bonus action. The clone can make one melee attack using your spell attack bonus, dealing 1d6 + your spellcasting ability modifier bludgeoning damage. 

The clone appears almost identical to you but feels gritty to the touch. A creature can determine it’s an illusion with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC.
