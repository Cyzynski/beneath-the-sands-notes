*Level 2 Abjuration (Druid, Ranger, Artificer)*
*(From dndbeyond content drops)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Touch
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a willing creature. Until the spell ends, the target's skin hardens into a tortoise shell, and the target gains a +3 bonus to its AC. If the target moves, the bonus becomes +1 instead until the start of its next turn.
