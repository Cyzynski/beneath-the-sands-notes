*Level 2 Conjuration (Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From @me.mimic)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Self (15-foot cube)
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You unleash a strike of draconic energy. Choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder for the type of strike. Each creature in a 15-foot cube originating from you makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d12 damage of the chosen type. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d12 for each slot level above 2nd.
