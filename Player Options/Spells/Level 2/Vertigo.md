*Level 2 Necromancy (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 60 ft.
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** 10 minutes

You mentally unbalance a creature within range, instilling within them a queasy sense of clumsiness. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become Poisoned for the duration. While Poisoned in this way, the creature treats all terrain as Difficult Terrain.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd-level or higher, you can affect an additional creature for each level above 2nd.
