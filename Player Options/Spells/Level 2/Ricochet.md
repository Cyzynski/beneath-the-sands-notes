*Level 2 Divination (Bard, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Bonus Action
**Range.** Self
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 2 rounds

By peering into the arcane ballistics of your weapon, you
cause the next ranged weapon attack you make to ricochet to an additional target within 30ft of the original. Make an attack roll against the new target, even if it is outside of the attack’s normal range. All targets hit take the base damage of the attack used.

***At Higher Levels.*** You can make an additional attack
against another target for each level above 2nd. The new target must be within 30 feet of the most recent target and it cannot have already been a target of this spell.
