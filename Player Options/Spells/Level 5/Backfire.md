*Level 5 Abjuration (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
*(From Many Worlds Tavern)*

**Casting Time.** Reaction
**Range.** 120 ft.
**Components.** V, S, M (A handheld mirror worth at least 100 GP)
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You attempt to interrupt and reverse a spell in the process of being cast. If a creature is casting a spell of 3rd level or lower, the spell backfires, as detailed below. If it is casting a spell of 4th level or higher, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability. The DC equals 8 + the spell's level. On a success, the creature's spell fails and has no effect. If your ability check exceeds the DC by 5 or more, the spell instead backfires. 

When a spell backfires, its target is changed to the caster. If the spell has an area of effect, it is centered on the caster, and if the area of effect was a line or a cone, it instead extends in the opposite direction intended by the caster. If the spell requires a saving through, the caster receives disadvantage on the saving throw. 

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the sell automatically backfires if it is 2 less than the level of the spell slot you used to cast this spell.
