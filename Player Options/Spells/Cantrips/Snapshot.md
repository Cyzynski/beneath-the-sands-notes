*Conjuration Cantrip (Bard, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Self
**Components.** S, M (A tray of flash powder and a piece of paper or parchment worth at least 1 CP, which the spell consumes.)
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You capture a snapshot of your surroundings, conjuring a
photograph that displays a perfect replica of what was in your vision during the moment you cast this spell. The photograph appears on the piece of paper or parchment used in the casting, has 1 Hit Point and Vulnerability to Fire damage.
