*Evocation Cantrip (Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 60 ft
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You hurl a crystalline shard of concentrated sunlight at a Creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 radiant damage. If the target is in darkness or dim light, the shard explodes on impact, dealing additional 1d4 damage to the target and 1d4 radiant damage to all creatures within 5 feet of the target.

***At Higher Levels.*** The damage increases by 1d8 and 1d4 respectively, when you reach 5th level (2d8 | 2d4), 11th level (3d8 | 3d4), and 17th level (4d8 | 4d4).
