*Conjuration Cantrip (Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 60 ft
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** 1 round

You create a small whirlwind of dust and debris at a point you can see within range. Each creature within 10 feet of the point must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5 feet away from the center and take 1d4 of bludgeoning damage. 

The dust devil extinguishes unprotected flames and disperses gases or vapors in a 10-foot radius. 

If cast in an area with loose sand or dust, creatures that fail their save are also blinded until the end of their next turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** The spell’s damage increases when you reach higher levels. This damage increases by 1d4 at 5th level (2d4) and 11th level (3d4) and 17th level (4d4).
