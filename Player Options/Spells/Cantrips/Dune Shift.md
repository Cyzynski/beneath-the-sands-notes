*Transmutation Cantrip (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 30 ft
**Components.** S, M (a handful of sand)
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You manipulate sand and loose earth within range. You can move up to 5 cubic feet of sand from one location to another within range, create or smooth a 5-foot square of difficult terrain, or create a small pit 5 feet across and 2 feet deep.

If you target the ground beneath a creature’s feet, they must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have their movement speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of their next turn.
