*Necromancy Cantrip (Cleric, Druid, Warlock, Wizard)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** 1 minute
**Range.** Touch
**Components.** V, S, M (A stoppered vial of salt or vinegar)
**Duration.** 1 week

You touch a piece of food or other edible object, preserving it from natural decay. For the duration, this object cannot spoil, rot, or disintegrate due to the passage of time or other natural forces. Once this spell is cast on an object, that object cannot be targeted by this spell again.

***At Higher Levels.*** The spell duration increases when you reach levels 5 (1 month), 11 (1 year), and 17 (forever).
