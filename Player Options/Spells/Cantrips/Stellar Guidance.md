*Divination Cantrip (Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Self
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You attune yourself to the positions of celestial bodies, gaining supernatural guidance. You know the direction to true north and can estimate the time of day to within an hour. 

Additionally, you have advantage on your next Wisdom (Survival) check made to navigate or track. This spell has no effect if cast underground or in areas where the sky cannot be seen.
