*Evocation Cantrip (Bard, Cleric, Wizard)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** Reaction (which you take when targeted by an attack)
**Range.** Self
**Components.** S, M (A polished piece of metal)
**Duration.** Instantaneous

In response to being targeted by an attack roll, you flash a piece of metal with blinding light. The attacker must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have Disadvantage on the attack roll. Creatures that can’t see you, or creatures that attack you while you are within range of their Blindsense or Tremorsense, automatically succeed on the saving throw.
