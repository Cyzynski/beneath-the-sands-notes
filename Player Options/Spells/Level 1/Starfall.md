*Level 1 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 action
**Range.** 90 feet
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You call down three motes of starlight that strike targets within range. Make a separate ranged spell attack for each mote. Each mote deals 1d4 + your spellcasting ability modifier radiant damage. 

You can direct the motes at the same target or different ones.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you create one additional mote for each slot level above 1st.
