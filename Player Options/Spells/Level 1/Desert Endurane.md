*Level 1 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 action
**Range.** Touch
**Components.** V, S, M (a drop of water)
**Duration.** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature and grant them resilience against harsh conditions. For the duration, the target has advantage on Constitution saving throws against exhaustion and requires only half the normal amount of water

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
