*Level 1 Conjuration (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 60 ft
**Components.** V, S, M (a grain of sand)
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You create a spear of crystallized sand and hurl it at a target. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 piercing damage, and the spear shatters into sharp fragments, dealing 1d4 piercing damage to all creatures within 5 feet of the target.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the primary damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
