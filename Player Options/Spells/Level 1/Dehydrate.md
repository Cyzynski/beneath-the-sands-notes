*Level 1 Necromancy (Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 60 ft
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You attempt to drain moisture from a creature you can see within range. The target must make a Constitution saving throw.
*Failure:* the target takes 2d6 necrotic damage and suffers one level of exhaustion. *Success:* the target takes half damage and doesn’t gain exhaustion. 

Plants and creatures with the water subtype have disadvantage on the saving throw

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
