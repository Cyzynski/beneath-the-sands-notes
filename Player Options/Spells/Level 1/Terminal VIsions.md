*Level 1 Necromancy (Bard, Cleric, Wizard)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Touch
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You envision the last moments of a corpse you touch. You experience the last minute of that corpse’s life through its senses; feeling, hearing, seeing, smelling, and tasting everything the corpse did within that time. Illusions and other magic appear real in the vision, unless the creature could see their true nature. If the creature had Darkvision or Truesight the vision gains the benefit of those special senses.

The minute-long vision coincides with the spell's duration. If the spell ends for any reason, the vision stops at that precise moment. The spell fails if the corpse was the target of this spell within the past 10 days. 

If you Concentrate on the spell for the full minute, you experience the pain of the creature’s death, taking 1d10 Psychic damage and gaining a Heroic Inspiration.
