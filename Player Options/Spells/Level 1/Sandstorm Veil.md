*Level 1 Evocation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Self (15-foot radius)
**Components.** V, S, M (a handful of sand)
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create a swirling veil of sand around yourself. The area within 15 feet of you becomes lightly obscured, and creatures other than you have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks while in the area. 

Creatures that start their turn in the veil must make a Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the start of their next turn.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the radius increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 1st.
