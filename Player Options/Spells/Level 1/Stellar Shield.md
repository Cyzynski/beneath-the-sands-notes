*Level 1 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 reaction, which you take when you would take damage
**Range.** Self
**Components.** V, S
**Duration.** 1 round

You call upon starlight to protect against harm. You gain resistance to the triggering damage and shed dim light in a 10-foot radius until the end of your next turn. 

If cast during nighttime or in an area where stars are visible, you also gain 1d4 + your spellcasting ability modifier temporary hit points.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the temporary hit points increase by 1d4 for each slot level above 1st.
