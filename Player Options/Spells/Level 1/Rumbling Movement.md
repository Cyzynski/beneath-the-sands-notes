*Level 1 Enchantment (Paladin, Ranger, Warlock)*
*(From @me.mimic)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Bonus Action
**Range.** Self
**Components.** S
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You move up to half your speed, walking along the ground. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks and each creature within 5 feet of you as you move must make a Constitution saving throw or be knocked prone.

***At Higher Levels.*** You move up to your whole speed instead if cast with a spell slot of level 3 or higher.
