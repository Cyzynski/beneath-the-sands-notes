*Level 1 Transmutation (Bard, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Bonus Action
**Range.** Self
**Components.** S
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You imbue a series of motions with such swiftness they appear instantaneous. When you cast this spell choose it to target either a weapon you are carrying, or yourself. When you target a weapon, you can immediately make one weapon attack with the targeted weapon against a creature or object within range. When you target yourself, you can immediately make one Utilize action.

The motions taken with this spell are too fast to be
perceived by most creatures. If you attempt to keep the motion hidden, make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check with advantage. If you roll higher than a creature’s passive Wisdom (Perception) score the action goes unnoticed. You cannot hide the motion from a creature targeted by one of the spell’s weapon attacks.
