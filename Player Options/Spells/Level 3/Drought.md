*Level 3 Necromancy (Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** 150 ft
**Components.** V, S, M (a withered plant)
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You create an area of supernatural dehydration in a 30- foot radius. When a creature starts its turn in the area, it must make a Constitution saving throw. 

*Failure:* the creature takes 2d6 necrotic damage and gains one level of exhaustion.
*Success:* the creature takes half damage and doesn’t gain exhaustion. 

Water in the area evaporates and plants wither unless magically protected.

***At Higher Levels.*** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.
