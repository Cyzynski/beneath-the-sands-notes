*Level 3 Abjuration (Artificer, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer)*
*(From Forgotten Sands)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Touch
**Components.** V, S, M (a handful of sand mixed with oil)
**Duration.** 1 hour

You touch a willing creature and surround them with protective sand that hardens like armor. The target’s AC becomes 16 + their Dexterity modifier (maximum 2). Additionally, when a creature hits the target with a melee attack, the attacker takes 1d6 slashing damage from the razor-sharp sand particles.
