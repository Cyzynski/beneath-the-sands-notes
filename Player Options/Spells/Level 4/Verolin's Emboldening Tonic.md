*Level 4 Abjuration (Paladin, Sorcerer, Warlock)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** Bonus Action
**Range.** Self
**Components.** V, S, M (A bottle of high-quality spirit worth 15+ GP, which the spell consumes)
**Duration.** 1 hour

You imbibe a powerful tonic fortified with with warding magic that numbs you to pain. You gain 30 Temporary Hit Points. While you retain any number of these, you automatically succeed on any Constitution saving throws you make to maintain Concentration, even if the damage you take is greater than the number of remaining Temporary Hit Points. If you replace these with any other Temporary Hit Points, the spell ends.

***At Higher Levels.*** You gain an additional 5 Temporary Hit Points for each spell slot level above 4th.
