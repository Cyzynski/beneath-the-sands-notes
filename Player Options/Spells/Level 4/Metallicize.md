*Level 4 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin)*
*(From @me.mimic)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Self
**Components.** V, S, M (a tine steel rod)
**Duration.** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Your skin gradually turns into hardened metal. When you cast this spell and at the start of each of your subsequent turns, your AC increases by 1 for the duration. For every 2 AC you gain this way, your movement speed is reduced by 5 ft.
