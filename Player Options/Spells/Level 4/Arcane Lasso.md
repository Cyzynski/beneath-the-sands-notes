*Level 4 Conjuration (Ranger, Wizard)*
*(From Gunslinger's Guide to Rexfald)*

**Casting Time.** Bonus Action
**Range.** 15 feet
**Components.** S, M (A cord of rope)
**Duration.** Concentration, 10 minutes

As a bonus action, you conjure an arcane lasso and attempt to bind a creature within 15 feet. Make a ranged spell attack, on a hit, the creature becomes Grappled by you for the duration. A creature Grappled in this way has Disadvantage on attack rolls. The arcane lasso has 10 hit points and Immunity to all non-Fore damage. A target Grappled by this spell can use its Action to make a Strength check against your Spell Save DC, freeing itself on a success.
