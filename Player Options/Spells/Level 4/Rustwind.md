*Level 4 Evocation (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
*(From @me.mimic)*

**Casting Time.** 1 Action
**Range.** Self, 30 ft. Cone
**Components.** S
**Duration.** Instantaneous

You unleash a blast of rusty air. Each creature in a 30-foot cone originating from you makes a Constitution saving throw.
*Failure:*  4d6 Acid damage and -2 AC for 1 minute.
*Success:* Half damage only.

A Construct or a creature wearing metal armor has disadvantage on this save. The AC reduction cannot stack.

***At Higher Levels.*** The area of the cone increases by 10 feet for each spell slot level above 4th.
