*Prerequisite: Level 4+*
*(From @me.mimic)*

You do not allow enemies to dictate distance. Once you commit, escape becomes a mistake. You gain the following benefits:

***Ability Score Improvement.*** Your Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution increases by 1 (to a maximum of 20).

***Quick Rise.*** When you are prone, you can stand up by expending your bonus action instead of half your movement.

***Pursuit.*** When a hostile creature you can see moves out of your reach, you can use your reaction to move up to your speed following that creature.
